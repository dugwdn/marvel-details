    Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2 trivia

    Background details, easter eggs and behind-the-scenes facts fans have spotted in Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2.

    Spotted on screenScene detailsMCU connectionsComics references

    Spotted on screen

    • Star-Lord tracks the Abilisk His tracker is a Mattel Electronics Classic Football handheld, with Mattel Electronics faintly visible on top. Reported by looper.com
    • Star-Lord's shirt The alien lettering on his shirt was decoded by a Reddit user as Gears Shift. Spotted by a Reddit user (not named), reported by looper.com
    • Ego's expansion on Earth Gregg Henry, Peter's grandfather from the first film, is seen escaping Ego's goo. Reported by looper.com
    • Stan Lee with the Watchers Stan Lee tells the Watchers about his time as a FedEx man, his Civil War cameo. Reported by looper.com
    • Crash landing on Berhert A Hulk-sized footprint is in the forest. Reported by looper.com
    • Yondu's scene on the snowy planet Contraxia Howard the Duck sits relaxing with a drink as the camera passes. Reported by looper.com
    • Dairy Queen scene James Gunn's parents appear, credited as Weird Old Man and Weird Old Man's Mistress. Reported by looper.com
    • Yondu's funeral Stakar Ogord, Charlie-27, Aleta Ogord and Martinex, the original comic Guardians, come to pay respects. Reported by looper.com
    • Yondu's funeral pyre A small blue crystal frog sits among the ornaments on the pyre. Reported by looper.com
    • Groot fetches things for Rocket Groot brings back a Ravager's fake eye, which Rocket keeps as a joke. Reported by looper.com
    • Ego reforms himself Ego briefly turns into David Hasselhoff. Reported by looper.com

    Scene details

    • James Gunn provide the dancing motions for the animation of Baby Groot in the opening titles.
    • The couple that is seen on Earth looking at Ego's erupted seed are James and Sean Gunn's parents, Jim Gunn Sr. and Leota Gunn.
    • The film was announced a week before the release of Guardians of the Galaxy at San Diego Comic-Con 2014 to be released on July 28, 2017. On October 28, 2014, the date was moved to May 5, 2017.
    • James Gunn had intended to feature a mention of an alien race called Sneepers in the film, but Marvel's legal team informed him that he could not as the word "sneeper" means "clitoris" in Icelandic. They later recalled the decision, allowing Gunn to put at least one Sneeper in the movie. The song Guardians Inferno is credited to a band called The Sneepers, featuring David Hasselhoff.
    • Prior to his death, James Gunn and Kevin Feige discussed the possibility of having David Bowie make a cameo appearance in the film.
    • This film is the first sequel in the Marvel Cinematic Universe to use a number rather than a subtitle since Iron Man 3.
    • Underneath the "SPACESUITS FOR EMERGENCY" instructions on the Milano, there are letters scribbled on that spell "OR FOR FUN". These words were written by James Gunn himself.
    • Matthew McConaughey was offered a role in the film, but turned it down in order to play Walter Padick in The Dark Tower.
    • The Marvel Studios logo animation introduced in Doctor Strange has been modified to incorporate footage from it.
    • Designs on floors and cushions on Ego's Planet are reminiscent of brain cells, referencing that Ego is a brain.
    • This is the first film to not include a character introduced in Phase One other than the Watcher Informant.
    • James Gunn has stated that the concept of Stan Lee being an informant for the Watchers was based on the fan theories that he was a Watcher.
    • James Gunn admitted that he forgot that Vol. 2 took place in 2014 when he settled on Stan Lee saying he was a FedEx employee, referencing his appearance in 2016's Captain America: Civil War. However, Gunn stated that he was probably a FedEx employee on multiple occasions.
    • James Gunn and Stan Lee went through a list of all of Stan Lee's cameos when deciding what his character referenced. Several different versions of the scene were considered. They even discussed mentioning a time he was a DJ at a strip club, referencing his cameo in Deadpool, which would have been the first time that an X-Men movie was referenced by an MCU film.
    • Adam Warlock was originally supposed to have a large role within the movie; however, James Gunn decided there were already too many characters. Warlock would later appear in Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3, portrayed by Will Poulter.
    • This is currently the only Marvel Studios film to take place entirely within the past, being set in late 2014. Though Captain America: The First Avenger was set primarily during World War II, its beginning and endings scenes took place in the present.
    • A credits caption promises "The Guardians of the Galaxy will return".
    • Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2 is one of the only 8 MCU films not featuring Iron Man (along with Thor: Ragnarok, Black Panther, Captain Marvel, Spider-Man: Far From Home, Spider-Man: No Way Home, Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness and Black Panther: Wakanda Forever) that grossed $800 million worldwide.
    • The Turkish dubbing credits of this film on Disney+ are the only one of the dubbing credits to be stylized along with the normal credits when Turkish dubbing credits were added in July of 2022.
    • James Gunn specifically noted Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2 as a movie that was nearly exactly like the storyboards.
    • Arkon - Is a spoof of the 1982 epic fantasy movie Conan the Barbarian (1982 film). In the comics, Arkon is a warrior and the ruler of Polemachus who fought against the Avengers.
    • Tony Stark - Is a biopic about Tony Stark in the style of Danny Boyle's Steve Jobs.
    • Haxan 2 - Is an action horror movie and hypothetical sequel of Swedish-Danish silent film Häxan.
    • Toxic Janitor 2 - Is a spoof of the 1989 Troma film The Toxic Avenger II that was directed by Lloyd Kaufman, James Gunn's friend and mentor.

    MCU connections

    • The Battle of Xandar is referenced several times in the movie.

    Comics references

    • Characters of the main cast introduced in Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2 adapted from the comics are Mantis, Ego, Ayesha and Taserface. Other characters adapted from the comics are Stakar Ogord, Tullk, Brahl, Martinex, Charlie-27, Aleta Ogord, Krugarr and Mainframe.
    • Ego's plants seem to be an amalgamation of Celestial Eggs and Ego's spore form from the comics. Celestial Eggs were eggs that were planted within planets that would eventually hatch Celestials. In the comics, Ego's spore form allowed him to grow on other planets and feed on them in order to rejuvenate.
    • Ego's humanoid avatar bears resemblance to Ego Prime.
