    Thunderbolts* trivia

    Background details, easter eggs and behind-the-scenes facts fans have spotted in Thunderbolts*.

    Spotted on screenScene detailsMCU connectionsBehind the scenes

    Spotted on screen

    • Alexei's home Red Guardian propaganda posters cover his place, and he watches old footage of himself beside Stalin at a parade. Reported by screenrant.com
    • Mel, Valentina's assistant Mel wears a round necklace with a bird in the middle, which may hint at the comics hero Songbird. Reported by looper.com
    • Closing title The Thunderbolts* logo vanishes and The New Avengers appears in its place. Reported by looper.com
    • End credits Jon Watts is in the Special Thanks; he was director Jake Schreier's college roommate. Reported by looper.com
    • Yelena blows up the OXE lab Before the lab blows, Yelena saves a guinea pig running a maze, foreshadowing the team running through their shame rooms. Reported by screenrant.com
    • Valentina's impeachment hearing Valentina names Wakanda and Talokan as rogue nations with vibranium. Reported by screenrant.com
    • Marvel Studios opening The fanfare warps and black shadows swallow the logo's color, foreshadowing the Void. Reported by screenrant.com and looper.com

    Scene details

    • The second poster for Thunderbolts* is based on a traditional box of Wheaties.
    • Following the Battle at the Watchtower, Hannah John-Kamen forgot to give Winter Soldier's Prosthetic Arm back to Sebastian Stan, causing the latter to ask for it. This blooper was kept in the final film.

    MCU connections

    • Valentina in the tower Valentina pours a drink behind the bar as the team confronts her, like Tony with Loki in The Avengers. Reported by looper.com
    • OXE vault John Walker says on your left; Wyatt Russell says the line was added later in ADR, so whether it is a planned callback is unclear. Reported by looper.com and cbr.com
    • Congressman Bucky talks to the press Bucky is the congressman for Brooklyn, where he and Steve grew up. Reported by screenrant.com

    Behind the scenes

    • Production on the film was halted due to the 2023 Writers Guild of America strike.
