    Black Widow trivia

    Background details, easter eggs and behind-the-scenes facts fans have spotted in Black Widow.

    Spotted on screenScene detailsMCU connectionsComics references

    Spotted on screen

    • Opening, the family home in Ohio An episode of DuckTales is playing on the living room TV. Reported by buzzfeed.com
    • Opening car chase in Ohio The cars chasing the family carry the 1990s S.H.I.E.L.D. logo. Reported by buzzfeed.com
    • Opening credits The credits use WHiH World News footage about a Russian sleeper cell in Ohio and a fire at the North Institute. Reported by buzzfeed.com
    • Ross hunts Natasha Ross lists Barton, Wilson and the incredible shrinking convict, leaving out Wanda and forgetting Scott Lang's name. Reported by buzzfeed.com
    • Taskmaster studies fighters Taskmaster watches footage of Hawkeye and Black Panther fighting at the Civil War airport. Reported by buzzfeed.com
    • Natasha hiding in Norway Natasha watches the 1979 Bond film Moonraker and quotes along with it. Reported by buzzfeed.com
    • Throughout Natasha wears an arrow necklace, which Scarlett Johansson chose as a nod to Hawkeye. Reported by buzzfeed.com
    • Budapest safe house A poster for the opera Die Vogel (The Birds) hangs behind Yelena, a hint that the Red Room is in the sky. Reported by buzzfeed.com
    • Budapest safe house Games of tic-tac-toe and hangman from Natasha and Clint's stay are on a duct; the hangman word is butterfly with B, T and F filled in. Reported by buzzfeed.com
    • Prison arm wrestling Alexei has tattoos of Melina's face with her name and of Natasha's and Yelena's names, and his knuckles read Karl and Marx. Reported by buzzfeed.com
    • Prison arm wrestling The inmate who breaks Alexei's wrist is Ursa, named after the comic werebear Ursa Major. Reported by buzzfeed.com
    • Melina's farmhouse A silver mask in Melina's weapons closet looks like the mask of Iron Maiden, a Black Widow rival in the comics. Reported by buzzfeed.com
    • Family dinner reunion Everyone sits in the same seats they had at dinner in 1995. Reported by buzzfeed.com
    • Yelena's vest The vest Natasha wears through Infinity War is Yelena's, and Yelena teases her about how much she likes it. Reported by buzzfeed.com
    • Ending, Rick Mason's delivery The Quinjet Rick gives Natasha is the one she flies in Infinity War. Reported by buzzfeed.com
    • Opening credits home videos Home footage shows Christmas, Easter and other holidays; Natasha later says they were all filmed in one day. Reported by buzzfeed.com

    Scene details

    • Black Widow is the third film to be set as a prequel (the first being Captain America: The First Avenger and the second Captain Marvel).
    • This is the first female-led solo film to open a Phase (4) in the Marvel Cinematic Universe.
    • This is the first solo film centered around a character who is deceased in the current MCU timeline.
    • This is the second female led superhero film from Marvel Cinematic Universe after Captain Marvel.
    • The film was released on July 9, 2021, making the longest gap between two films since The Incredible Hulk and Iron Man 2, as Spider-Man: Far From Home was released on July 2, 2019, making a gap of over 24 months between Black Widow and Far From Home. It also marks the longest gap between the last film of an MCU phase and the first film in another phase.
    • On October 7, 2020, it was revealed that Disney and McDonald’s have teamed up to offer a new collection of Marvel Studio toys as part of the McDonald Happy Meals.The line up will feature the likes of Falcon, Winter Soldier, Wanda Maximoff, Vision, Groot, Hulkbuster, Wasp, Hulk and the Black Widow herself. This merchandise of toys is also tied to promotion for WandaVision and The Falcon and The Winter Soldier, two Disney+ shows that premiered in January and March 2021, respectively.
    • On October 18, 2020, Dan Loeb, the hedge fund manager for Third Point, suggested having high-profile releases on Disney+ instead. His argument laid in Disney+ needing to attract more subscribers with more content, otherwise they would always be in Netflix's shadow; Loeb placed a $1 billion stake in the company. Thus, he believes that the only way to keep the story going is to release Black Widow on the streaming site instead.
    • On November 5, 2020, several new images from the recently released book, Marvel's Black Widow: The Official Movie Special appeared online and gave a glimpse actor O-T Fagbenle's somewhat mysterious character Rick Mason. This was further expanded when O-T Fagbenle himself said on Black Widow: The Official Movie Special Book that "Mason is a finder for people who aren't so affiliated with armies, like people who are part of the underworld. He can find anything you need as well. He's had a relationship doing that work for the Black Widow, and they have a bit of a vibe going on. There's a sense that there's more to their relationship than it being purely work-related, so that's always in the mix"
    • On November 9, 2020, Epic Games teased that the next Marvel skin that would be available for free in the subsequent Marvel Knockout Series cup would be the Snowsuit Black Widow skin.
    • On November 16, 2020, Ray Winstone shared some insight into the man in charge of the Red Room as well as his ties to David Harbour's Red Guardian. Winstone started off by saying in the Black Widow: The Official Movie Special that Dreykov is "a guy who came out of old Russia after the breakdown of communism. He really wanted to change something and wanted to protect his nation."; He elaborated further by saying how he thinks that Dreykov likely "was a man who started off with great intentions, but like most great men who are put into a situation, the greed and the power takes over. The Black Widow program is his. The Widows are part of his team. They're his ninjas."; As for what connection he has with Russia's version of Captain America, Winstone explains that "The Red Guardian was created as a Captain America, but a Russian version. He's a big powerful dude, who certainly started off believing in Russia. So, if America is making a bomb, then Russia has got to make a bigger bomb. Well, you have Captain America, who is s quite a good-looking guy. And he has got all the gear."
    • On December 15, 2020, news began to circulate that Marvel Studios is beginning to explore new talent deals to provide flexibility in case the pandemic or other factors lead the studio to bypass theaters and take the films straight to Disney+. This would mean that under the new contract language, key above-the-line talent like actors, writers, directors and producers will receive adjusted compensation depending on whether the film opens in theaters or debuts on the Disney+ streaming service.
    • On December 23, 2020 it was revealed through a release on Weibo, Marvel Studios president Kevin Feige announced that a Marvel-themed musical that would premiere on Bilibili (a Chinese video-sharing website) on December 31,2020 to celebrate the new year and what the MCU has in store for Phase 4.
    • On December 27, 2021, Patty Jenkins who has directed Wonder Woman and Wonder Woman 1984 for the DCEU respectively, revealed via social media that she'd relish the chance to helm more superhero movies in the coming years. When asked which franchise she'd choose if she had the choice, Patty said on Twitter: "Thank you for the question. Probably Superman, Spiderman or Black Widow! So much potential with all of them..."
    • On January 3, 2021, Milla Jovovich gushed about her daughter, who was cast as a young Natasha, and made everyone cry on set while filming Black Widow. Jovovich went onto say that, "We call her Baby Widow. She's such a fan of the Marvel movies and you know, Ever is just such an incredible talent. She's just a real natural and it was so wonderful to watch her on set...I mean, literally, she had everybody crying during one of her scenes! She's amazing. I think people are going to be really, really impressed when they see the movie next year."
    • On January 18, 2021, Kevin Feige would go on to acknowledge the unexpected similarities between The Blip and the COVID-19 pandemic, before reiterating that he wants any future references to Thanos' attack to "have more meaning behind it". As a result, Phase Four, would instead be focused on setting up new stories rather than looking back to past events.
    • On February 24, 2021, Disney President of Marketing Asad Ayaz discussed the promotional plan for Black Widow after the film's prolonged delay. When it was pointed out that Disney hasn't restarted the promotion for the movie ever since the shift in schedule, Ayaz shared that the last thing that they want to do in this situation is to overmarket the marketing campaign for the MCU prequel. However, Ayaz did reveal that his team will be introducing original materials when the marketing for Black Widow restarts as it gears up for its theatrical debut in May 2021. It was also unveiled that Disney will be taking a fresh approach in terms of selling the movie to viewers.
    • On February 25, 2021, Tom Holland revealed that Marvel still hasn't let him see the Black Widow film and it isn't for a lack of trying on his part. He mentioned how he has asked to view the film, bothering Marvel in recent weeks in the hopes they'll set up a screening. But Marvel has yet to do so and Holland finishes by calling out to Marvel in the hopes that, if they're listening, they'll finally let him see the film.

    • On March 9, 2021, Florence Pugh released two behind the scenes stills of her with Scarlett in their costumes for the Black Widow film. Also on this day, it was reported at Disney’s annual shareholder meeting by CEO Bob Chapek continued to underscore that the release date for Marvel’s long-awaited feature Black Widow is May 7 will stick and that will be seen in theaters. Chapek made the same confirmation back on Feb. 11 during the studio’s fiscal Q1 earnings call.
    • On March 13, 2021 that Marvel Studios Assembled, will also be used that this documentary type series will be utilized for a behind-the-scenes look at Marvel Studios' first film of Phase 4, Black Widow, specifically mentioning the film's star Scarlett Johansson.

One such example was the announcement that Black Widow will move back to July and will be available simultaneously in cinemas and on Disney Plus.

This came three months after its theatrical release, and it's available on Disney+ starting July 9, 2021 with Premiere Access for those who pay the $29.99 price tag.

    MCU connections

    • Taskmaster fights Taskmaster copies Cap's shield moves, Black Panther's claws, Bucky's knife flip and Hawkeye's bow. Reported by buzzfeed.com
    • Post-credits, Yelena's dog Yelena names her dog Fanny, one of Rick's fake names for Natasha. Reported by buzzfeed.com
    • On November 8, 2020, Scarlett confirmed that the film would finally address what happened in Budapest, which was first teased back in 2012's The Avengers in a conversation that Black Widow and Hawkeye had.
    • On November 9, 2020, David Harbour teased that Natasha and Alexei have been working together on and off since the 1990's as their bonds have strengthened with each other. Harbour also revealed how close his character is with Nat, once rescuing her from the Red Room but later throwing her back into the fire.
    • On November 16, 2020, it was mentioned that because the film is set between the events of Captain America: Civil War and Avengers: Infinity War, Black Widow will find herself without the support of S.H.I.E.L.D. or the Avengers, Black Widow will have to get supplies and vehicles for her mission through O.T. Fagbenle's Rick Mason.
    • On November 30, 2020, reports began to circulate that Florence Pugh has reportedly join the cast of Hawkeye.
    • On February 2, 2021, Jac Schaeffer, who serves as the head writer and creator of WandaVision, says it was more of a challenge to write the Black Widow film, which she also was part of the team, than Marvel's first series.
    • On February 16, 2021 it was revealed that Chloé Zhao, who is directing and did some writing for the Eternals film, was at one time, in the running for directing the Black Widow film.
    • On March 1, 2021, storyboards for WandaVision, which featured a Black Widow reference were leaked. It specifically referenced Black Widow's death and lack of funeral to honor her at the end of Avengers: Endgame.
    • On April 28, 2021, a new featurette released by Marvel in celebration of National Superhero Day for the Black Widow film was released on this day with Scarlett Johansson asking the audience how much they know about her Black Widow character.
    • On May 13, 2021, it was revealed that a current release in theaters remains unclear and would likely be impacted one way or the other by how films like Black Widow and Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings do.
    • On June 16, 2021, Scarlett mention in an interview that she had been waiting for Marvel boss Kevin Feige to green-light a solo film since The Avengers rocked the box office in 2012. However, she further revealed that she was glad the film didn't come into fruition until recently, as it would have been a very different movie that the final version audiences will see on July 9, 2021. Also on this day, Scarlett also mentioned how proud she felt that her character of Black Widow has evolved from being hyper sexualized all the way back in Iron Man 2, to a more evolved and fully developed Natasha in her standalone film.
    • On June 15, 2021, a promotional video for Yelena Belova was released by Marvel Studios.
    • On June 19, 2021, Kevin Feige said that "exploring the past, present, and future of the MCU is certainly in the cards" during a recent press conference. Which hints at Black Widow film not be the only prequel the MCU may have in store for audiences.
    • On July 3, 2021, Shortland hinted that the Black Widow film franchise could continue with a sequel revolving around a different character.
    • On July 7, 2021, it was announced that Disney's Marvel Studios films and MCU series won’t be putting in an appearance during the virtual edition of Comic-Con @ Home.
    • On July 9, 2021, the post-credits scene has Yelena Belova being approached by Contessa Valentina Allegra de Fontaine who previously appeared last in The Falcon and The Winter Soldier. Moreover, it appears that Yelena has been carrying out missions for the Contessa, with her latest target being none other than Hawkeye. Which in turn sets up Florence Pugh's confirmed appearance on Hawkeye.
    • On October 6, 2021, it was revealed that while Marvel’s What If...? season 1 has just concluded, the creative team behind What If…? teased that for the second season, Head writer A.C. Bradley said that “Going into the second season, we're sticking with anthology form, and it's going to be all-new stories, lots of fun, new heroes, and pulling more from Phase Four than we were obviously able to this season. Hopefully, we'll see hints of the Eternals and Shang-Chi and the Black Widow film characters too.

    • The film marks the MCU debuts of Yelena Belova, Alexei Shostakov/Red Guardian, Melina Vostokoff/Iron Maiden, Rick Mason and Taskmaster.
    • During their conversation in Melina's house, Yelena mistakenly refers to Alexei as the "Crimson Dynamo," the name of an Iron Man supervillain in the comics.
