    I Am Groot trivia

    Background details, easter eggs and behind-the-scenes facts fans have spotted in I Am Groot.

    Scene detailsMCU connectionsComics referencesBehind the scenes

    Scene details

    • The Marvel Studios opening introduction is the first to fast forward though it.
    • Each episode has a different notice about the different animals that were not harmed during the production.
    • Groot's First Steps is the only episode to take place prior to the main events of Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2.

    MCU connections

    • Groot reads a book called Dumps Happen with a cover of Thanos in Groot's Pursuit, a parody of the book Everyone Poops.
    • On the back of the Alf comic that Groot reads in Magnum Opus, an advertisement for Pingo Doce, a soda product from Brazil introduced in The Incredible Hulk.

    Comics references

    • This episode The Little Guy introduces the Grunds, a species from the comics that gains energy from solar radiation.
    • In the episode Magnum Opus, Groot reads Alf #4, a real life Marvel Comic.

    Behind the scenes

    • This is James Gunn's third role in the Marvel Cinematic Universe after his roles as the Maskless Sakaaran in Guardians of the Galaxy and Groot in Guardians of the Galaxy, Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2, and The Guardians of the Galaxy Holiday Special. Gunn followed this portrayal up with his fourth role as Lambshank in Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3.
    • These are the first shorts ever to be produced by Marvel Studios.
