    Doctor Strange trivia

    Background details, easter eggs and behind-the-scenes facts fans have spotted in Doctor Strange.

    Spotted on screenScene detailsMCU connectionsComics referencesBehind the scenes

    Spotted on screen

    • Strange's broken watch after the car crash His watch reads February 2, a little after 5:15, so the crash happened on Groundhog Day. Director Scott Derrickson said he had waited two years for someone to notice. Spotted by Twitter user @TimDLydon (quoted by Scott Derrickson), reported by screenrant.com
    • Strange's room in Kamar-Taj Mordo hands Strange a paper reading Shamballa; it's not a mantra, it's the wifi password. Reported by screenrant.com and looper.com
    • Strange speeding before the crash The song in his car is Pink Floyd's Interstellar Overdrive; a Pink Floyd album cover once used Doctor Strange art. Reported by screenrant.com
    • Strange takes a call while driving He turns down a 35-year-old Air Force colonel who crushed his spine in experimental armor; it sounds like Rhodey, but the filmmakers said it was not a War Machine reference. Reported by screenrant.com and looper.com
    • Strange takes a call while driving Another case is a 22-year-old woman with an electronic implant in her brain who was struck by lightning. Reported by screenrant.com and looper.com
    • New York skyline and the Mirror Dimension Avengers Tower with its blue A is in the skyline and folds with the other towers in the Mirror Dimension. Reported by screenrant.com and looper.com
    • Strange arrives at Kamar-Taj The old man Strange mistakes for the Ancient One is Master Hamir, who has no hands. Reported by screenrant.com
    • Stan Lee on a bus Stan Lee is reading Aldous Huxley's The Doors of Perception. Reported by looper.com
    • New York Sanctum The Bleecker Street Sanctum's number is 177A. Reported by looper.com
    • First fight with Kaecilius Strange reaches for an axe on the wall, but the Cloak of Levitation pulls him toward a magic weapon instead. Reported by looper.com

    Scene details

    • In Unbelievable Gwenpool Vol 1 #3, Doctor Strange and Gwenpool look at a theater where Doctor Strange is showing in the Multiverse. The theater had simplified versions of a couple of the posters used for the film. Doctor Strange is pleased with the choice of Benedict Cumberbatch to portray him.
    • Originally, Scott Derrickson intended to make Nightmare the main villain of the movie, but Kevin Feige was against it. “Kevin made a very cogent case,” said Derrickson. “The trouble with starting with Nightmare is getting across the idea of the Dream Dimension as another dimension. The movie was challenging enough. It's already an exposition-heavy movie... Dormammu made the most sense. And he is the most present villain in the comics.”
    • The property master Barry Gibbs revealed that the Ebony Blade was meant to appear in the movie, alongside the other relics conserved in the New York Sanctum. "That was going to go into a book called the Lexicon of Relics, but the Ebony Blade was taken out of the script. I don't quite know why. Again, as things evolved, it wasn't pertinent to the script. Things just moved on."
    • In an early draft of the movie, Stephen Strange was meant to become the Sorcerer Supreme by the end of the movie, but Derrickson and his crew had many many problems with that.
    • In concept art for the Chamber of Relics the Screaming Idol was featured.
    • Rachel McAdams' startled reaction in the broom closet of Metro-General Hospital was genuine, since the mop handle fell completely by accident, and scared McAdams almost out of her skin. Scott Derrickson decided to keep the reaction in the film, since she never broke character.
    • A post-credits caption promises "Doctor Strange will return".

    MCU connections

    • Wong makes a comparison between the Masters of the Mystic Arts and the Avengers to Stephen Strange, specifying that they are battling more mystical threats from extra-dimensional beings.
    • The Avengers Tower can be seen on the New York City skyline.
    • Near the end of the movie, Wong reveals that the Eye of Agamotto contains an Infinity Stone. This is the fifth Infinity Stone revealed in the Marvel Cinematic Universe.
    • The Guidebook to the Marvel Cinematic Universe - Doctor Strange reveals that Stephen Strange and Christine Palmer worked at Metropolitan General Hospital, which is also called Metro-General Hospital. This hospital franchise was introduced in Daredevil, and later appeared in Jessica Jones and Iron Fist.

    Comics references

    • Characters from the movie adapted from the comics in the main cast are Stephen Strange/Doctor Strange, the Ancient One, Karl Mordo, Kaecilius, Christine Palmer, Wong and Dormammu. Other characters from the comics are Nic West, Daniel Drumm and Hamir. A character based on Tina Minoru, named in the credits only, has a cameo appearance in the film. Minoru later appeared in Runaways and played by Brittany Ishibashi, the show does not reference her cameo leaving it unclear if it is still the same person.
    • When he entered Kamar-Taj, Strange mistakes Master Hamir for the Ancient One, only to be greeted by the real one. This is a reference to the original Ancient One appearances in the comics.
    • The look of the Dark Dimension is heavily inspired by Steve Ditko's design of Eternity's realm in the comics.
    • Kamar-Taj's wi-fi password is "Shamballa", a reference to the mystical lost kingdom and, more specifically, to the 1986 graphic novel Doctor Strange: Into Shamballa.
    • The surgery scene of Doctor Strange operated by Christine Palmer at the Metro-General Hospital, is nearly identical to the surgery scene of the Doctor Strange: The Oath, where Strange is operated by Linda Carter.

    Behind the scenes

    • Colin Farrell, Jake Gyllenhaal, Tom Hardy, Ethan Hawke, Jack Huston, Oscar Isaac, Jared Leto, Matthew McConaughey, Ewan McGregor, Keanu Reeves and Benedict Cumberbatch were all considered for Stephen Strange. Farrell previously portrayed Bullseye in the 20th Century Studios production Daredevil. Gyllenhaal later played Quentin Beck/Mysterio in Spider-Man: Far From Home. Hardy later played Eddie Brock/Venom in the Columbia Pictures productions Venom, Venom: Let There Be Carnage and will return for Venom: The Last Dance, he also had a cameo in Spider-Man: No Way Home due to Spider-Man and Doctor Strange breaking the multiverse. Hawke later played Arthur Harrow in Moon Knight and Isaac played Moon Knight in the same show. Leto later played Michael Morbius/Morbius in the Columbia Pictures production Morbius. McGregor later portrayed Roman Sionis/Black Mask in the DC Extended Universe production Birds Of Prey.
    • Benedict Cumberbatch was the first choice and was offered the lead role of Stephen Strange but initially had to decline due to his theatre commitments playing Shakespeare's Hamlet which will clash with the film's proposed shooting date. Joaquin Phoenix was the studio's second choice for lead role but Phoenix, after a few months of deliberation, eventually turned down the offer due his reluctance to do a multi-picture deal that was required by the studio if he signed on.
    • Co-Writer C. Robert Cargill revealed on his podcast Junkfood Cinema that he suggested cameos for Jeffrey Combs and the late Dick Miller but ultimately was unsuccessful in securing them. Combs had previously starred in the film Doctor Mordrid which started as a Doctor Strange film.
    • The film has been in development since 1986. Wes Craven was set to direct in 1992. Later in 2001, David S. Goyer was going to write and direct. In 2008, Guillermo del Toro was brought on as director. In 2014, Mark Andrews, Jonathan Levine, Nikolaj Arcel, Scott Derrickson and Dean Israelite were on the shortlist to direct with Derrickson being the ultimate choice.
    • This is the second film to feature Doctor Strange after a live-action TV movie featuring Peter Hooten in the lead role in 1978 that premiered on CBS, which was airing The Amazing Spider-Man and The Incredible Hulk TV series at the time.
    • This film introduces a new Marvel Studios logo, now incorporating elements from previous films into the animation. Like Thor: The Dark World, the previous film to introduce a new studio logo, it is the second film in its respective phase.
