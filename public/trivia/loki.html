    MCU connections

    • The first scenes of Glorious Purpose consist of mostly reused footage from Avengers: Endgame.
    • When Miss Minutes talks about the multiverse in her presentation during Glorious Purpose, she mentions how a nexus event gone wrong could cause the multiverse to branch out into "madness", subtly hinting to the events of Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness, which the series connects to.
    • Loki's arrival in the Gobi Desert parallels Iron Man's landing after escaping the Ten Rings in Iron Man. Upon arriving, Loki recites the speech he made at the start of The Avengers.
    • Later in the episode, he watches footage from his previous actions in Thor and The Avengers discovers what happened to his main timeline counterpart by watching footage from Thor: The Dark World, Thor: Ragnarok, and Avengers: Infinity War.
    • In The Variant, Loki discovers that the actions of the Revengers caused Ragnarök, as depicted in Thor: Ragnarok. He later uses this event to attempt to explain his theory about Sylvie Laufeydottir's location to Mobius M. Mobius.
    • Loki's landings in The Variant, after being punched by Sylvie, and Lamentis, after jumping off a train, are similar to his landing in Thor: Ragnarok after Doctor Strange brings him back from a dimension where he fell for half an hour.
    • In Glorious Purpose, Loki's final words to Mobius and Sylvie are, "For you. For all of us", which is the last words he said to Odin in Thor before he fell into a wormhole.
    • In the film Deadpool & Wolverine, when Deadpool sees Alioth in the Void, he mentions the latter's appearance in Loki: Season One, Episode Five: Journey Into Mystery.

    Comics references

    • The series features the MCU debut of Marvel Comics characters Ravonna Renslayer, Mobius M. Mobius, and the Time-Keepers. It also features the MCU debut of the Time Variance Authority.
    • In Glorious Purpose, one of the TVA's structures has the term "T-282" on it. This is a nod to The Mighty Thor #282, which featured an incarnation of Kang the Conqueror known as Immortus. Later, when the tape about Loki's life ends, it is shown to be labelled "ETH-616", a reference to Earth-616, which is the main continuity in the comics.
    • The song that Sylvie Laufeydottir plays at the start of the episode is "Holding Out for a Hero" by Bonnie Taylor, which was featured in the comic Vote Loki #2. Additionally, the scene takes place in Oshkosh, Wisconsin, which was the home of former Marvel Comics writer and editor Mark Gruenwald, whom the TVA staff in the comics were modelled after due to his grasp on continuity.
    • Later, in Ravonna Renslayer's office, a collection of relics of past missions from agents under her command is seen, much like how Kang the Conqueror, Renslayer's love interest in the comics, keeps a collection of "trophies" from his past victories.
    • As Loki reads files in the TVA's library, a floor with the number 372 can be seen, a nod to The Mighty Thor #372, the first appearance of the Time-Keepers.
    • Renslayer's number during her time as a Hunter was A-23, a reference to the comic Avengers #23, in which she made her debut.
    • The Citadel at the End of Time resembles Doctor Doom's castle from the 2015 Secret Wars event.
    • He Who Remains mentions he and his variants were allies before the Multiversal War, a reference to the Council of Kangs from the comics.

    Episode titles

    • Glorious Purpose has two meanings: It references the speech Loki made after being transported to Earth in The Avengers, which he tries to repeat at the start after arriving in the Gobi Desert; It also alludes to Loki's overall opinion of himself, which is shattered throughout the episode as he discovers the Time Variance Authority's nigh-omnipotence and his lack of free will. Loki repeats the episode title to Hunter B-15 before their fight, after he discovers his original timeline self's death at the hands of Thanos; It is also the first title in the Marvel Cinematic Universe to be used twice in the same show.
    • The Variant references Sylvie Laufeydottir, a Variant of Loki who had mainly been referred to as "The Variant" prior to her name being revealed.
    • Lamentis is the name of the planet whose moon Sylvie are Loki are trapped on in the episode.
    • Journey Into Mystery is the name of the comic book series in which Loki, Sif, Thor, Korg (through a retcon) and several other characters made their debuts.
