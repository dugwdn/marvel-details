    Cloak & Dagger trivia

    Background details, easter eggs and behind-the-scenes facts fans have spotted in Cloak & Dagger.

    Scene detailsMCU connectionsComics references

    Scene details

    • Cloak & Dagger is the first MCU installment to have both male and female co-leads and have the names of two characters in the title, the second being Ant-Man and the Wasp. Coincidentally, both products were released in 2018.

    MCU connections

    • The Roxxon Corporation, after having minor appearances throughout the Marvel Cinematic Universe, is featured extensively for the first time, as it shows up as an antagonist in the first season of Cloak & Dagger. Roxxon's competition with Stark Industries and Rand Enterprises is mentioned; Additionally, it is mentioned that Roxxon also operates in Pensacola. This is a reference to Roxxon's role in the events of Iron Man 3.
    • The Darkforce, previously established in Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. and Agent Carter, is featured in the show. The second season sees the Dark Dimension featured, with a specific domain inhabited by the Loa.
    • Brigid O'Reilly, who was mentioned in Luke Cage, references her times being in New York City, as well as her friend Misty Knight and Ben Urich.
    • The drink brand "O'Harren's Scotch Whisky" appears in both seasons of the show, after previously appearing in Agent Carter, Daredevil and The Punisher.
    • A New York Bulletin article about Luke Cage written by Karen Page is shown and talked about.

    Comics references

    • Cloak & Dagger marks the debuts of Tyrone Johnson/Cloak and Tandy Bowen/Dagger in the MCU. Other characters adapted to Cloak & Dagger from comics include Brigid O'Reilly, Francis Xavier Delgado, Otis Johnson, Adina Johnson, Melissa Bowen, and Mister Jip. Season two added comics characters D'Spayre and Avandalia.
    • In First Light, Tandy Bowen uses a T-shirt that nods to the comic book counterpart of the famous British Band the Beatles, the Crick-Hits.
    • August D'Angelo is mentioned as Roxxon's CEO in an article shown in the episode Princeton Offense.
    • The show references elements from Vodu, such as Papa Legba and Baron Samedi. These two deities have been featured in Marvel Comics. The show also uses the song "the hymn to St. Brigid" which is a tribute to Maman Brigitte, as well as St. Bridget, the Irish Saint that Maman Brigitte comes from. Joe Pokaski has confirmed that it is also connected to Brigid O'Reilly.
