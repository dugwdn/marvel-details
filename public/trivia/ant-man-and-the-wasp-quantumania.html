    Ant-Man and the Wasp: Quantumania trivia

    Background details, easter eggs and behind-the-scenes facts fans have spotted in Ant-Man and the Wasp: Quantumania.

    Scene detailsMCU connectionsComics referencesBehind the scenes

    Scene details

    • This is the 31st film in the Marvel Cinematic Universe, which is also the same amount of ice cream flavors Baskin-Robbins has.
    • This is the first Marvel Studios opening introduction to include a villain, He Who Remains.
    • Scott Lang's book Look Out for the Little Guy! was produced as a real life book and is available for purchase.
    • A design easter egg to Rama-Tut was put in the Moon Knight episode The Friendly Type.
    • This is the first Ant Man film to not feature Michael Pena as Luis in any capacity.
    • This is the first MCU film where Loki appears without Thor himself.

    MCU connections

    • Scott Lang and Jimmy Woo go out for lunch, a joke was made about them going to dinner in Ant-Man and the Wasp.
    • Scott Lang returns to Baskin-Robbins, his place of employment from the first Ant-Man.
    • Christine Everhart and WHiH Newsfront are mentioned as reviewers of Look Out for the Little Guy!, the first time WHiH Newsfront has been mentioned outside of WHiH Newsfront, a web series whose first season was promotion for the first Ant-Man film.
    • The events of the Blip from Avengers: Endgame are mentioned when Cassie Lang gets out of jail.
    • The events of the Time Heist, as well as Hulk and Rocket are mentioned in Scott Lang's book, Look Out for the Little Guy!.
    • The events of the Avengers Civil War from Captain America: Civil War are mentioned by Scott Lang.
    • Darren Cross returns after being presumed dead from the the first Ant-Man.
    • Thor and Mjølnir are mentioned by Victor Timely when he imprisoned Scott and Cassie Lang.
    • Incursions, a concept first introduced in Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness is mentioned by Victor Timely.
    • The post-credits scene includes Loki and Mobius M. Mobius, and Victor Timely, setting up Loki season two.

    Comics references

    • The Ant-Man Suit and the Wasp Suit worn by Scott Lang and Hope van Dyne more accurately reflect the ones from the Marvel Comics.
    • Sub-Atomica is mentioned, in the comics, Subatomica is a solar system within the Microverse.
    • Krylar is a character from the comics who only appeared in one comic issue as a citizen of the planet Ky.
    • The city of Axia is similar to Kang the Conqueror's city of Chronopolis from the Marvel Comics.
    • This is the first appearance of Rama-Tut, Immortus, Mister Gryphon, and Victor Timely, all four Kang the Conqueror variants, as well as the Council of Kangs from the Marvel Comics.
    • The first appearance of the Council of Kangs matches 1988's Avengers #292 in which there is an auditorium full of Kangs.

    Behind the scenes

    • This is Ruben Rabasa's second role in the Marvel Cinematic Universe after his role as Cardozo in the Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. episode Who You Really Are.
    • This is David Dastmalchian's third role in the Marvel Cinematic Universe after his roles as Kurt Goreshter in Ant-Man and Ant-Man and the Wasp, and an alternate version of Kurt Goreshter in the What If...? episode What If... Zombies?!.
    • This is Katy M. O'Brian's second role in the Marvel Cinematic Universe after her role as Kimball in the Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. episodes Brand New Day, The End is at Hand, and What We're Fighting For.
