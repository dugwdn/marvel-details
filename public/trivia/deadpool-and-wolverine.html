    Deadpool & Wolverine trivia

    Background details, easter eggs and behind-the-scenes facts fans have spotted in Deadpool & Wolverine.

    Spotted on screenScene detailsMCU connectionsBehind the scenesPop culture

    Spotted on screen

    • Fight with the Deadpool Corps A store sign reads Liefeld's Just Feet, a joke about co-creator Rob Liefeld's drawings of feet; the store gets wrecked. Reported by looper.com
    • Bus fight A Stanley Steamer ad on the bus shows Stan Lee's face. Reported by looper.com
    • X-23 joins the fight Laura puts on the pink flowered sunglasses she wore in Logan. Reported by looper.com
    • Fight inside the Honda When Logan is pushed against the car radio, a snippet of The Greatest Show from The Greatest Showman plays. Reported by looper.com
    • The Void The Void holds the crumbling 20th Century Fox logo, Red Skull's Hydra car, the Moon Knight cupcake truck, the Milano, a giant dead Ant-Man and a Chitauri Leviathan. Reported by looper.com and cbr.com
    • Wade's interview with Happy Hogan Happy's office has the Mark II helmet, the toy Iron Man mask young Peter wore in Iron Man 2, a photo of Tony with Peter, Captain America trading cards and an early shield prototype. Reported by looper.com
    • Cassandra Nova's lair Doctor Strange's Cloak of Levitation hangs in her throne room, and she uses a sling ring. Reported by looper.com and cbr.com
    • Deadpool searches other worlds Huey Lewis and the News' The Power of Love plays, a Back to the Future song. Reported by looper.com
    • Opening credits NSYNC's Bye Bye Bye plays as Deadpool fights TVA agents with Logan's skeleton. Reported by looper.com
    • Deadpool Corps Nathan Fillion voices Headpool and Matthew McConaughey voices Cowboy Deadpool. Reported by looper.com
    • TVA soldiers and Deadpool variants Rob McElhenney is a helmeted TVA soldier, and Wrexham striker Paul Mullin plays a Welsh-flag Deadpool. Reported by looper.com
    • Deadpool Corps Ryan Reynolds' children play Kidpool and Babypool, their first roles in his films. Reported by looper.com

    Scene details

    • Deadpool & Wolverine is the first Marvel Cinematic Universe film to serve as a sequel to a non-MCU film based on Marvel Comics characters, as Deadpool and Deadpool 2 are part of the X-Men film series produced by 20th Century Fox.
    • This is the first MCU film where none of the main characters originate from the MCU Universe. This is also the first Deadpool film to be officially set in the Marvel Cinematic Universe.
    • This film is the first ever MCU film to target an "R" (Restricted 18+) rating from the Motion Picture Association of America, in line with the prior two Deadpool films. It is also the second MCU movie to drop the f-bomb uncensored, following Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3, in which Star-Lord dropped it at Nebula while telling her to open the car door.
    • This is the first Deadpool film to not feature T.J. Miller as Weasel in any capacity.
    • Multiple titles were considered for the film, originally named Deadpool 3, during shooting including Deadpool vs. Wolverine, Deadpool & Wolverine (which ended up being the film's title) and Deadpool 3 with Wolvie.
    • Hugh Jackman said "Fuck Yeah!" when he tried the iconic Wolverine Suit for the first time.
    • A crucial scene from the film was supposed to be a shot-for-shot recreation of Luke Skywalker and Darth Vader's final duel from Return of the Jedi.
    • In response to a series of set leaks from the film, Ryan Reynolds posted humorously edited versions of the set leaks. Edits include adding a Predator, Mickey Mouse and Steve Urkel to certain photos and replacing the 20th Century Fox logo with the logo of Quibi.
    • In the movie, the comic book Secret Wars #5 can be seen next to Deadpool in the Void before he is met by an angry Wolverine.
    • Vinnie Jones, who played Cain Marko/Juggernaut in X-Men: The Last Stand, rejected an offer to reprise the role in Deadpool & Wolverine over complaints regarding the suit and the lack of budget for it from Marvel Studios.
    • Karan Soni revealed that the original story for Deadpool & Wolverine back when it was being developed as Deadpool 3 at 20th Century Fox similarly brought back Wolverine but involved Deadpool traveling to the North Pole to save Christmas.
    • The film has been announced to contain lots of "Kevin Feige jokes".
    • Famke Janssen, who played Jean Grey/Dark Phoenix in the X-Men film series, has declined to be in the film after being asked about whether she will reprise her role or not in the film several times beforehand.
    • Deadpool & Wolverine features Dogpool making it the second MCU project that includes an animal variant of a character. The first project to include this was Loki, which featured Alligator Loki and Throg.
    • Deadpool & Wolverine features Kidpool, making it the second MCU project that includes an kid variant of a character. The first project to include this was Loki, which featured Kid Loki.
    • Deadpool & Wolverine is the first MCU film in which a director/writer and a star/writer receive full producer credits.
    • Disney+ released a green band version of the official teaser for Deadpool & Wolverine with a few notable differences, including: An added scene with Peter Wisdom carrying Wade Wilson's birthday cake before setting it on a table; Wade Wilson says "The R-rating isn't new for me" instead of "Pegging isn't new for me" before he is arrested by TVA Minutemen; This scene, involving Rob McElhenney (a friend of Ryan Reynolds) playing said Minuteman, was later cut entirely from the film; Deadpool yells "OMG" instead of "Oh my fuck" after witnessing a Minuteman being snatched from the ground by Alioth.
    • The establishment Liefield's Just Feet is a reference to Deadpool creator Rob Liefeld, who has a reputation of being bad at drawing feet. In July 2023, Liefeld approved the joke before it was included in the movie.
    • Ryan Reynolds' original pitch for Deadpool & Wolverine was rejected by Kevin Feige as he wasn't sure how to incorporate Deadpool in the MCU. Reynolds pitched at least 25 different versions of Deadpool & Wolverine.
    • Kevin Feige initially told Hugh Jackman to not return as Wolverine after the character's death in Logan. However, Feige was onboard for Jackman reprising the role after it was decided he would not be playing the same variant of Wolverine he played in the Fox X-Men series.
    • On May 30, 2024, Ryan Reynolds revealed on X that a popcorn bucket had been created for the film that resembled Wolverine's head, with popcorn being poured into his mouth. He declared that 2024 would be remembered as the year that the War of the Popcorn Buckets began, referencing the infamous popcorn bucket created for the release of Dune: Part Two, which was modeled after a Sandworm and inspired many jokes that it would be used as a sex toy.
    • Following the announcement of Disney shifting physical media distribution to Sony Pictures Home Entertainment, this is the first Marvel Studios feature film to not be distributed by Walt Disney Studios Home Entertainment on physical media in North America.
    • During the early development of Deadpool & Wolverine, Mephisto was one of the antagonists considered for the film.
    • The fact that Ladypool had recently had a baby in the film is a reference to the fact that Blake Lively, the actress who portrayed Ladypool, had recently had a baby.
    • Deadpool told a Minuteman to "get [his] country's name out [his] fucking mouth", a reference to what Will Smith told Chris Rock at the 94th Academy Awards after the former slapped the latter.
    • Channing Tatum wanted to take Gambit's costume with him at the end of filming, as he had done on his previous films, but was too scared to do so.
    • While this movie primarily serves a farewell tribute to mostly Fox-produced Marvel movies, Wesley Snipes' Blade, an alternate Punisher and an variant of Russian are the only characters who are neither from Marvel Cinematic Universe Prime, nor from alternate universe movies produced by 20th Century Fox in this movie. Blade's case is from New Line, which is now merge into Warner Bros. Pictures. Although it is unknown which alternate version of Punisher referred in this movie, due to the character being dead prior to the main event, while Russian is confirmed to be a Variant of the one from Earth-58732.
    • A "What's up, Doc?" line spoken by Gambit is a reference to iconic greetings spoken by Bugs Bunny from Warner Bros.' Looney Tunes.
    • In order to get Dogpool to lick Deadpool's mouth, Ryan Reynolds put salmon paste on his lips.

    MCU connections

    • After the Sabretooth fight Deadpool holds up Sabretooth's head and calls it the head of Furiosa, joking about the Mad Max look of the Void. Reported by looper.com
    • When confronted by Hulk in an alternate Wolverine's universe, Deadpool begins saying he is Marvel Jesus and calls Hulk a "dull creature" before he is swiped away by the behemoth, referencing how Loki said he was a god and called Hulk a dull creature in The Avengers.
    • Deadpool and Wolverine get shawarma after destroying the Time Ripper and defeating Cassandra Nova and Mr. Paradox, similar to how the Avengers got shawarma after the Battle of New York in The Avengers.
    • The opening intro's song "Bye Bye Bye" by NSYNC was featured in X2: X-Men United, when Pyro turns on the radio in Cyclops' car and Wolverine annoyingly turns it off. Way back in Deadpool 2, NYSNC was suggested to call by Deadpool after Celine Deon's performs Ashes.
    • In Deadpool 2, The music box has Logan's corpse, in Logan is the burial grave where Laura buried Logan, and in the movie is where Deadpool desecrated to exhume Logan's skeleton.
    • A certain part of the fight choreography of the Duel in the Void is an exact recreation of the fight between Peter Parker and Flash Thompson in Spider-Man.
    • Blade says that "some motherfuckers [were] still trying to ice skate uphill", a line he originally said in Blade.
    • When Deadpool gets Nicepool killed due to the latter not having a healing factor, Wolverine calls Deadpool God's perfect idiot, which is what Deadpool was called in the opening credits of 20th Century Fox's Deadpool.
    • Deadpool mentions Scoutmaster Kevin, a character he originally mentioned in Deadpool 2.
    • Blade says he doesn't like Deadpool, to which Deadpool responds that he never did, referring to Wesley Snipes. This a reference to the fact that Snipes and Ryan Reynolds did not enjoy working together on Blade: Trinity.
    • Deadpool attempts to console Elektra about the death of her world's Daredevil at the hands of Cassandra Nova, to which she replies, "It's fine." This is a joke simultaneously referencing the 20th Century Fox film Daredevil, which starred Ben Affleck as Matt Murdock alongside Jennifer Garner as Elektra, and the relationship between the two co-stars that emerged from their work together on the film which ended when the pair divorced in 2018.
    • Deadpool and his friends celebrate his return by eating Feige's Famous Pizza, a pizza place that Deadpool ordered from in Deadpool.
    • During the credits, a montage of footage and archive footage from Marvel movies made by 20th Century Fox is shown to honor the legacy of those movies. Featured in the montage includes Hugh Jackman as Wolverine, Patrick Stewart as Professor X, Ian McKellen as Magneto, Halle Berry as Storm, James Marsden as Cyclops, Chris Evans as Human Torch, Jennifer Garner as Elektra, Scott Adkins as Weapon XI, James McAvoy as Professor X, Anna Paquin as Rogue, Rebecca Romijn as Mystique, Jennifer Lawrence as Mystique, Stan Lee, Kelly Hu as Lady Deathstrike, Alan Cumming as Nightcrawler, Kodi Smit-McPhee as Nightcrawler, Liev Schreiber as Sabretooth, Tyler Mane as Sabretooth, Jessica Alba as Invisible Woman, Ryan Reynolds as Weapon XI and Deadpool, Ben Affleck as Daredevil, Jon Favreau as Foggy Nelson, Brian Cox as William Stryker, Michael Fassbender as Magneto, Vinnie Jones as Juggernaut, Famke Janssen as Jean Grey, Doug Jones as Silver Surfer, Tye Sheridan as Cyclops, Aaron Stanford as Pyro, Michael B. Jordan as Human Torch, Dafne Keen as X-23, Nicholas Hoult as Beast, Miles Teller as Mister Fantastic, Michael Chiklis as Thing, Jamie Bell as Thing, Sophie Turner as Phoenix, Evan Peters as Quicksilver, and Oscar Isaac as Apocalypse.

    Behind the scenes

    • Deadpool sees a crying Thor The Thor shot is reused footage from Loki's death in Thor: The Dark World with Deadpool edited in; Chris Hemsworth shot nothing new. Reported by looper.com

    Pop culture

    • Cavillrine unleashes his claws by pumping or "reloading" his arms, mimicking Henry Cavill's character in Mission: Impossible – Fallout, August Walker, doing the same motion in preparation for a fight.
    • The Nakatomi Plaza from Die Hard is present among the stuff that had been pruned to the Void by the Time Variance Authority.
    • The Chrysler LeBaron from Planes, Trains and Automobiles is present among the stuff that had been pruned to the Void by the Time Variance Authority.
    • Deadpool asks Dogpool who she wants to be her owner: the "original recipe", referring to himself, or "Van Milder," referring to Nicepool. This is a reference to the film Van Wilder, in which Ryan Reynolds portrayed the titular character.
    • Deadpool puts his hand on the glass of the door to the matter and anti-matter chamber in a manner that called back the way Spock placed a Vulcan salute on a chamber door in Star Trek II: The Wrath of Khan.
    • Nicepool says “the proposal” to the audience during the confrontation with the Deadpool Corps, The Proposal was another movie Ryan Reynolds starred in.
