    WHiH Newsfront (web series) trivia

    Background details, easter eggs and behind-the-scenes facts fans have spotted in WHiH Newsfront (web series).

    MCU connectionsComics references

    MCU connections

    • WHiH Newsfront is an extension of the Marvel Cinematic Universe fictional television network WHiH World News. The organization was introduced in The Incredible Hulk and Iron Man 2.
    • Christine Everhart, who previously appeared in Iron Man and Iron Man 2, stars in the series.
    • The first season of WHiH Newsfront served as marketing for the film Ant-Man. Scott Lang and Darren Cross makes their first appearances in the MCU. Hank Pym appears via pictures; Pym Technologies and Robbery of VistaCorp are discussed; Additionally, the first season also references the events of Avengers: Age of Ultron. Everhart discusses the Battle of Sokovia, while several characters from the movie appears via pictures.
    • The second season of WHiH Newsfront served as marketing for the film Captain America: Civil War. Events from The Avengers, Captain America: The Winter Soldier, and Avengers: Age of Ultron are discussed. Footage and pictures of several members of the Avengers are featured; Events from the third season of Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. are mentioned; The end of the webseries dovetails with the Attack in Lagos. President Matthew Ellis appears to discuss the consequences of the initial events of Civil War; Doctor Strange is also referenced.

    Comics references

    • A WHiH reporter, Jackson Norris, is based on the Marvel Comics character of the same name. The Marvel One-Shot: All Hail the King character Jackson Norriss is based on the same character.
