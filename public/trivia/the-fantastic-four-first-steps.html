    The Fantastic Four: First Steps trivia

    Background details, easter eggs and behind-the-scenes facts fans have spotted in The Fantastic Four: First Steps.

    Spotted on screenScene detailsComics referencesBehind the scenes

    Spotted on screen

    • The Ted Gilbert Show The arrows on the TV stage match the ones on stage when the Beatles played The Ed Sullivan Show on February 9, 1964. Reported by cbr.com
    • Background signage A sign reads Westview Appliances, a nod to WandaVision's town (director Matt Shakman also made WandaVision). Reported by cbr.com and screenrant.com
    • Rescue and news reports Actors from the unreleased 1994 Fantastic Four film appear: Michael Bailey Smith and Jay Underwood as rescued workers, Alex Hyde-White and Rebecca Staab as reporters. Reported by cbr.com
    • The Ted Gilbert Show The team's ship is named the Excelsior, Stan Lee's catchphrase. Reported by cbr.com
    • Silver Surfer arrives Two young men at a studio window are surrounded by comic art, including a page marked Timely Comics and a Tales of Suspense #27 cover; the article reads them as Kirby and Stan Lee. Reported by cbr.com
    • United Nations An empty seat labeled Latveria, with a green flag, waits for Doctor Doom's country. Reported by cbr.com and screenrant.com
    • Final shot The film's universe is Earth-828, after August 28, Jack Kirby's birthday. Reported by cbr.com
    • After the post-credits scene A QR code leads to a Future Foundation-style Marvel.com page that unlocks Fantastic Four comics. Reported by screenrant.com
    • End credits The film is dedicated to Marvel Studios veteran Jamie Christopher and For Inez, director Matt Shakman's late mother. Reported by screenrant.com

    Scene details

    • Unlike all other MCU films, this movie has no connection to previous films and is the first film not to have any already established characters.
    • After saluting the street workers, the Human Torch uses his flames to make a 4 symbol in the sky, just like Chris Evans' Human Torch in the 2005 and 2007 films.

    Comics references

    • The Fantastic Four: First Steps marks the introduction of the eponymous team and its two members: Invisible Woman, and Thing in the MCU. Both Mister Fantastic and Human Torch variants have appeared in Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness and Deadpool & Wolverine respectively.
    • The Fantastic Four: First Steps also marks the introduction of H.E.R.B.I.E. from the Fantastic Four 1970's cartoons.
    • The Timely Comics office features comic book pages straight from the comics. They include appearances of Metallo (Tales of Suspense #16), Oog (Tales of Suspense #27), when the Fantastic Four fought Red Ghost and his super apes on the Blue Area of the moon and the debut of Uatu (Fantastic Four #13), Mister Fantastic entering the Negative Zone (Fantastic Four #51), and Thing and Human Torch in the Negative Zone (Fantastic Four Annual #6). They are all meant to pay tribute to Jack Kirby's work.
    • The Timely Comics office features a chalk board that reads "Big Unstoppable Monster!" This is likely a reference to early Tales of Suspense comics as well as some early issues of Journey into Mystery and Strange Tales comics that featured giant monsters. Those comics were made by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby in the late 1950s and 1960s, which is the same era Earth- 828 is in. It may also be a refence to Galactus being the main antagonist of the film.
    • The scene of the Timely Comics office may be a reference to Fantastic Four #10, the first time Stan Lee and Jack Kirby are shown working at Marvel. Their office has sketches of Hulk, Thor, and Ant-Man, and covers for Strange Tales, Tales to Astonish, and Tales of Suspense can be seen. Kirby is pitching a newly created villain, False-Face, when they are interrupted by the return of Doctor Doom who had been lost in space. Doctor Doom forced Lee and Kirby into trapping Mister Fantastic so that he could use body swapping technology he gained from the Ovoids since he had grown tired of wearing his mask and his scarred face.
    • At one point, it is theorized that Galactus could be a space dragon. This is likely a reference to the Makluans which are dragon-like aliens created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby in the Strange Tales comics. In the comics, they created what would become the Mandarin's Rings.
    • The scene of Igor stretching Reed's cheek from the credits was originally based on the cover of Fantastic Four Vol 7 22 by Alex Ross, where two Vampires are biting and stretching them. However, the final version of the scene that made it into credits is a bit different.
    • The post-credit scene featuring the The Fantastic Four Power Hour has the Fantastic Four running past a shop named Impossible Toys & Arcade, being a reference to Impossible Man and Arcade. The shop also has Arcade's signature yellow and red bow tie.

    Behind the scenes

    • This film is the fifth live-action incarnation of the team, following two different sets of movies produced by 20th Century Fox, the one in Marvel Cinematic Universe film Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness and one in 1994. However, the 1994 movie was unreleased.
    • The cast of the 1994 film: Alex Hyde-White (Mister Fantastic), Jay Underwood (Human Torch), Rebecca Staab (Invisible Woman), and Michael Bailey Smith (Thing) make cameos in the movie.
