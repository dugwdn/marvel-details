    Black Panther: Wakanda Forever trivia

    Background details, easter eggs and behind-the-scenes facts fans have spotted in Black Panther: Wakanda Forever.

    Spotted on screenScene detailsMCU connectionsComics references

    Spotted on screen

    • Opening logo The Marvel Studios logo plays with no sound, filled only with images of Chadwick Boseman as T'Challa. Reported by looper.com
    • Riri's car Riri's license plate reads EE-0710, likely pointing to Black Panther Vol. 7 #10. Reported by looper.com
    • Riri's dorm room Her bed has a Chicago Bulls pillow and a moon-phase wall hanging, and her earrings are tiny iron hearts. Reported by looper.com
    • Namor's name Namor's name is Roman spelled backward. Reported by looper.com
    • Okoye's new armor Shuri names the blue armor Midnight Angels, after a rebel group in the comics. Reported by looper.com

    Scene details

    • According to Ryan Coogler, the original script was different from the released script as it was written before the tragic death of Chadwick Boseman. It would have followed T'Challa, who was struggling after a five-year absence due to the Blip. T'Challa II would have been shown earlier in the film as T'Challa struggled to cope with the fact that he missed five years of his son's life. Additionally, Namor was still supposed to be the villain.
    • In the subtitles of the movie, different languages are displayed in different colors. Yucatec-Mayan is in blue, Xhosa is in yellow, and Spanish is in white.
    • The phrase "This Jade Tooth Bites" is engraved on the Megalodon jawbone used by Namor as his throne.

    MCU connections

    • Ross in Wakanda Shuri greets Ross with colonizer, the same nickname from their first meeting. Reported by looper.com
    • When Namora offers Shuri some clothes, Riri Williams remarks at how that trope is present in many movies. Shuri responds that Williams should see what she had to wear at Warrior Falls, which is a callback to Shuri's joke in Black Panther where she acts like she is challenging for the throne, but just complains about her corset.
    • In the screenplay, Shuri guesses that Riri Williams either works for DARPA, Roxxon Corporation, or Project P.E.G.A.S.U.S.

    Comics references

    • In the comics, Namor and the rest of his kingdom are Atlanteans. However, in the film, they are Talokanil. This was done to avoid similarities with Aquaman in the DCEU.
    • The Midnight Angel Armor closely resembles the Dora Milaje's current uniform in the comics.
    • When analyzing Namor, Shuri claims him to be as strong as the Hulk, a clear nod to Hulk and Namor's frequent fights in the comics.
    • During his final confrontation with Shuri, Namor says "Imperious Rex" in Yucatec, which in the comics is his main battle cry.
