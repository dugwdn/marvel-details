    Secret Invasion trivia

    Background details, easter eggs and behind-the-scenes facts fans have spotted in Secret Invasion.

    Spotted on screenMCU connectionsComics referencesBehind the scenes

    Spotted on screen

    • Opening, Prescod's conspiracy board A CIA document with a Skrull mugshot is labeled Treadstone, and notes list attacks including an Oktoberfest in Munich. Reported by looper.com and looper.com
    • Episode 1 news Chris Stearns appears on FXN, the news channel from Daredevil season 3. Reported by cbr.com
    • Skrull DNA lab Sample labels include Extremis, Groot and SPECIMEN SAMPLE: CULL, and screens read HTTP-616 and Sequencing Result 838. Reported by looper.com
    • Episode 4 A bench is engraved Carter 1933-2000, the first name hidden. Reported by looper.com
    • Episode 4 Fury brings Rhodey a bottle of Pappy Van Winkle bourbon. Reported by looper.com
    • Episode 3 flashback A title card dates the scene 1998 and Priscilla mentions Dreykov's men. Reported by looper.com
    • Episode 5, Fury's jet Rick Mason from Black Widow gives Fury a fake ID and says the Helicarrier has been mothballed. Reported by looper.com
    • Episode 1, Fury's Moscow safe house A poster advertises an Island Art show at the Brixton Community Gallery. Reported by looper.com

    MCU connections

    • The events of the Blip from Avengers: Endgame are mentioned by Talos.
    • This is the first appearance of James Ritson, the United States President first mentioned on a news ticker in Black Panther: Wakanda Forever.
    • The Battle of London from Spider-Man: Far From Home is mentioned by Sonya Falsworth.
    • This is the first series in the Marvel Cinematic Universe to not feature a post-credits scene.
    • The television news channel FXN from Daredevil was watched by Nick Fury and appeared with a segment called The Chris Stearns Show.
    • The track Be Your Enemy, as featured on Secret Invasion: Volume 2, although not featured within the final show itself, contains the leitmotif that played during Catastrophe from the Captain America: Civil War - Original Motion Picture Soundtrack in reverse, which was the track that played during War Machine's fall in the Battle at Leipzig-Halle Airport.

    Comics references

    • Secret Invasion introduces Pagon, a Skrull from the comics who briefly impersonated Richard Nixon.

    Behind the scenes

    • According to Ali Selim, Nick Fury in Secret Invasion takes inspiration from the classic American gunslinger.
    • This is Martin Freeman's second role in the Marvel Cinematic Universe following his role as Everett Ross in Captain America: Civil War, Black Panther, and Black Panther: Wakanda Forever.
    • This is Dermot Mulroney's first role in the Marvel Cinematic Universe following his contributions as a cellist for the scores in Spider-Man: Homecoming and Spider-Man: Far From Home.
    • This is Uriel Emil's first and second role in the Marvel Cinematic Universe portraying both Vasily Poprishchin and the Vasily Poprishchin Skrull.
    • This is Tony Curran's third role in the Marvel Cinematic Universe following his roles as Bor in Thor: The Dark World and Finn Cooley in the Daredevil episode Penny and Dime.
    • The introduction at the beginning of the episode was made up of AI art.
