    The Guardians of the Galaxy Holiday Special trivia

    Background details, easter eggs and behind-the-scenes facts fans have spotted in The Guardians of the Galaxy Holiday Special.

    Spotted on screenScene detailsMCU connectionsComics referencesBehind the scenes

    Spotted on screen

    • Opening credits The opening plays The Pogues' Fairytale of New York over comic art mixed with Christmas images, and ends with Marvel Studios spelled in red and green Christmas lights. Reported by looper.com
    • Knowhere Christmas party The band Old 97's appear as aliens and sing I Don't Know What Christmas Is (But Christmastime Is Here); Kevin Bacon later sings Here It Is Christmas Time with them. Reported by looper.com
    • Kevin Bacon's house Bacon's house number is 1988, the year Yondu took Peter Quill from Earth. Reported by looper.com
    • Kevin Bacon at home Bacon is watching Santa Claus Conquers the Martians, a film about aliens kidnapping a holiday figure, just before Mantis and Drax kidnap him. Reported by looper.com
    • The Guardians' new ship The new ship is the Bowie, named after David Bowie, following the Milano and the Benatar. Reported by looper.com
    • Hollywood Boulevard Banners and a billboard advertise Kingo's movie, hinting the Eternals star survived the end of Eternals; Kumail Nanjiani later thanked Gunn for the posters. Reported by looper.com and looper.com
    • TCL Chinese Theatre Mantis and Drax mistake a costumed street performer for Steve Rogers; impostors include Black Widow, Ant-Man, Captain Marvel, Thor and Jack Sparrow. Reported by looper.com
    • Souvenir shop A star map lists Queen Latifah, John Cena and Arnold Schwarzenegger, with a floating head of Mark-Paul Gosselaar. Reported by looper.com
    • Gift exchange Groot carves dioramas for everyone: Peter's shows Mantis and Drax chasing Bacon, and Kraglin's is a carving of Kraglin opening smaller Kraglins. Reported by looper.com
    • Yondu flashback A package addressed to Taserface in sloppy handwriting sits among the presents Yondu wrecked. Reported by looper.com

    Scene details

    • The Legends episodes prior to the holiday special, Drax and Mantis were taken down from Disney+ due to them spoiling the reveal that Mantis is Peter Quill's half-sister.
    • This is the only Guardians of the Galaxy project to not feature Zoe Saldaña (Gamora) in any capacity.

    MCU connections

    • Gift exchange Nebula gives Rocket a metal arm described as Bucky's, paying off Rocket trying to get the arm in Infinity War. Reported by looper.com
    • Two films starring Kingo are visible when Mantis and Drax visit Hollywood including Haxan 3: Haxaner and Kingo's Kristmas.
    • Nebula gifts Rocket Bucky Barnes' Vibranium arm, a call back to Avengers: Infinity War when Rocket asked Barnes about his arm.

    Comics references

    • The Marvel Studios opening introduction is changed to comic panels and covers of Christmas issues.

    Behind the scenes

    • The Beverly Hills Police Department Officer Fitzgibbon is named after James Gunn's personal friend, Larry Fitzgibbon. Gunn puts a character named Fitzgibbon in all of his films. This is the third character named Fitzgibbon in the Marvel Cinematic Universe after Doctor Fitzgibbon from Guardians of the Galaxy and Officer Fitzgibbon from Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2.
    • James Gunn pitched the idea of the holiday special to Kevin Feige while on the set of Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2.
    • This special was filmed alongside Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3.
    • This is the first Marvel Studios production to feature both live-action and 2D animation.
