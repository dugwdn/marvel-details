    The Guardians of the Galaxy Holiday Special trivia

    Background details, easter eggs and behind-the-scenes facts fans have spotted in The Guardians of the Galaxy Holiday Special.

    Scene detailsMCU connectionsComics referencesBehind the scenes

    Scene details

    • The Legends episodes prior to the holiday special, Drax and Mantis were taken down from Disney+ due to them spoiling the reveal that Mantis is Peter Quill's half-sister.
    • This is the only Guardians of the Galaxy project to not feature Zoe Saldaña (Gamora) in any capacity.

    MCU connections

    • Two films starring Kingo are visible when Mantis and Drax visit Hollywood including Haxan 3: Haxaner and Kingo's Kristmas.
    • Nebula gifts Rocket Bucky Barnes' Vibranium arm, a call back to Avengers: Infinity War when Rocket asked Barnes about his arm.

    Comics references

    • The Marvel Studios opening introduction is changed to comic panels and covers of Christmas issues.

    Behind the scenes

    • The Beverly Hills Police Department Officer Fitzgibbon is named after James Gunn's personal friend, Larry Fitzgibbon. Gunn puts a character named Fitzgibbon in all of his films. This is the third character named Fitzgibbon in the Marvel Cinematic Universe after Doctor Fitzgibbon from Guardians of the Galaxy and Officer Fitzgibbon from Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2.
    • James Gunn pitched the idea of the holiday special to Kevin Feige while on the set of Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2.
    • This special was filmed alongside Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3.
    • This is the first Marvel Studios production to feature both live-action and 2D animation.
