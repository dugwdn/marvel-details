    Ms. Marvel trivia

    Background details, easter eggs and behind-the-scenes facts fans have spotted in Ms. Marvel.

    Spotted on screenScene detailsMCU connectionsComics references

    Spotted on screen

    • Episode 2 school hallway A Coles Academic High School poster hides a QR code that opens a free Ms. Marvel comic. Reported by buzzfeed.com
    • Episode 2, Kamran offers driving lessons The neon signs behind Kamala change to a big pink heart. Reported by buzzfeed.com
    • Episode 2 bathroom Kamala wears a shirt with the comic Black Widow design, and later strikes Natasha's Iron Man 2 pose. Reported by buzzfeed.com
    • Episode 2 social media montage A tweet uses the hashtag duexmoi, spelled wrong and not shown as a link. Reported by buzzfeed.com
    • Episode 2 closing credits A Trust A Bro truck from Hawkeye appears. Spotted by Twitter user named Matthew, reported by buzzfeed.com
    • Episode 2 dream Kamala dances to The Ronettes' Be My Baby, a nod to Dirty Dancing. Reported by buzzfeed.com
    • Coles Academic High School A plaque lists the school's founders, who are the creators and editors of the Ms. Marvel comics. Reported by screenrant.com

    Scene details

    • Due to Disney+ being unavailable for streaming in Pakistan, the series was theatrically released in the country in three parts.

    MCU connections

    • Episode 2 Kamran says his mom has a crush on Kingo Senior, the Bollywood star from Eternals. Reported by buzzfeed.com
    • Tony Stark and Natasha Romanoff's sacrifice, as seen in Avengers: Endgame, is honored in a mural. The scene of Natasha's death is homaged in Episode 2, with Bruno and Kamala taking Clint and Natasha's place.
    • Several of the heroes present in Endgame are seen depicted as people in costumes.
    • A drawing of Captain America is shown, seemingly riffing off the Avengers: Endgame quote "That is America's ass".
    • A booth advertises tours to New Asgard, as will be featured in Thor: Love and Thunder.
    • P. Cleary returns after previously making his debut in Spider-Man: No Way Home. In the episode Crushed, he uses the same interrogation technique with Zoe Zimmer as he did with Ned Leeds; pretending to be intrigued by the things they've done before getting onto the topic of the interrogation.

    Comics references

    • Characters from Marvel Comics adapted in the show are Kamala Khan/Ms. Marvel, Aamir Khan, Muneeba Khan, Yusuf Khan, Kareem/Red Dagger, and Kamran. Additionally the show includes supporting characters from the comics run, such as Bruno Carrelli, Nakia Bahadir and Zoe Zimmer.
    • In the Coles Academic High School, there are nods to artists and comic book writers that have worked on Kamala Khan's comics, such as G. Willow Wilson, Ian Herring, Stephen Wacker, Takeshi Miyazawa, Adrian Alphona, Joe Caramagna, Jamie McKelvie and Nico Leon.
    • Kamala's guidance counselor Gabe Wilson, Jr. is also meant as a nod to G. Willow Wilson, one of Kamala's co-creators.
    • During the credits, artwork for both Nakia and Aamir's comic book design are shown when the names of their respective actors appear on screen.
    • There is an image of Carol Danvers' first suit as the original Ms. Marvel. This costume is also the one used by Zoe Zimmer at AvengerCon.
    • Kamala's clock features the artwork from the 2014 tradepaper back Captain Marvel Vol 1: Higher, Further, Faster, More.
    • Episode 1, Generation Why, refers to the story arc from Issues 8 to 11 from Ms. Marvel Vol 3.
    • Episode 2, Crushed, refers to the story arc from issues 13 to 15 of Ms. Marvel Vol 3.
