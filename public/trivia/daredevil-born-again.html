    Daredevil: Born Again trivia

    Background details, easter eggs and behind-the-scenes facts fans have spotted in Daredevil: Born Again.

    Scene detailsMCU connectionsBehind the scenesEpisode titles

    Scene details

    • Daredevil: Born Again is a sequel series to the show Daredevil, and as of 2026, is the only sequel series within the MCU.

    MCU connections

    • In the series premiere Heaven's Half Hour, Foggy Nelson references his former employment at Hogarth, Chao & Benowitz to Kirsten McDuffie.
    • While conversing with Matt Murdock at Square Diner in Heaven's Half Hour, Wilson Fisk mentions a past attempt to mentor someone, referencing Maya Lopez. Murdock alludes to how she shot Fisk in the face, gesturing towards the events of the Hawkeye episode So This Is Christmas? In the same conversation, Fisk reflects on the death of Foggy Nelson and acknowledges the promise he made not to expose Murdock's secret identity, nor target either Nelson or Karen Page, following his defeat at the Presidential Hotel in the Daredevil series finale A New Napkin.
    • Wilson Fisk's televised speech commemorating his election to Mayor of New York City references Spider-Man with regards to addressing his intent to curb vigilante activities within the city, alongside Daredevil and the Punisher in Heaven's Half Hour.
    • A billboard advertisement for Rogers: The Musical is seen in an establishing shot of Times Square in the episode Optics. Also seen in the same shot is an advertisement for Harlem's Paradise, the nightclub run by Luke Cage.
    • Yusuf Khan, featured previously in The Marvels and Ms. Marvel, appears in the episode With Interest. Kamala Khan is also referenced in the episode, and the family photo of Yusuf, Kamala, Muneeba Khan, and Aamir Khan is shown.
    • Skrulls are mentioned in the episode Sic Semper Systema, having previously had their existence made public in Secret Invasion.
    • FXN, previously featured in both Daredevil and Secret Invasion, makes an appearance in both seasons' premiere episodes.
    • From off-screen, Valentina Allegra de Fontaine makes a phone call to Alan Steverud while he meets with Wilson Fisk at New York City Hall during the second season premiere episode The Northern Star, successfully persuading him to pledge support for the Safer Streets Initiative and enable him to conduct his policies without broader federal oversight.
    • The Watchtower, the main base of operations for the New Avengers, appears in establishing shots of New York City in the second season episodes The Northern Star, Gloves Off, and The Hateful Darkness, having completed construction since the events of Thunderbolts*.
    • Ben Urich's office at New York Bulletin is shown in the second season finale The Southern Cross, featuring some of his newspaper articles from Daredevil. The articles also serve as references to the Battle of New York from The Avengers, the Duel of Harlem from The Incredible Hulk, Tony Stark and Stark Tower, and Cybertek from Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D..

    Behind the scenes

    • Vanessa Fisk, previously portrayed by Ayelet Zurer in Daredevil, was recast and portrayed by Sandrine Holt, who was later recast back to Zurer.
    • This is Michael Gaston's second role in the Marvel Cinematic Universe following his role as Gerald Sharpe in the Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. episode Alien Commies from the Future!.
    • This is Clark Johnson's first role in the Marvel Cinematic Universe following his contributions as a director for the two Luke Cage episodes You Know My Steez and For Pete's Sake.
    • Production on Daredevil: Born Again was delayed due to the 2023 Writers Guild of America strike.

    Episode titles

    • The title of episode 1.01, Heaven's Half Hour, is a reference to Josie's toast to Cherry, as well as the brief celebration and bliss for Nelson, Murdock & Page before Bullseye attacked Josie's Bar.
    • The title of episode 1.02, Optics, is a reference to Sheila Rivera explaining to Wilson Fisk the importance of optics in his first few days in office. The title may also be a reference to Matt Murdock's blindness.
    • The title of episode 1.03, The Hollow of His Hand, is a reference to Heather Glenn's toast to Matt Murdock on the verdict to The People v. Hector Ayala.
    • The title of episode 1.04, Sic Semper Systema, is a play on the Latin phrase "Sic semper tyrannis" ("thus always to tyrants"), instead referencing Matt Murdock's waning faith in the justice system.
    • The title of episode 1.05, With Interest, is a reference to the financial concept of interest, as the episode takes place in New York Mutual Bank.
    • The title of episode 1.06, Excessive Force, is a reference to Mayor Wilson Fisk enlisting corrupt cops into his Anti-Vigilante Task Force, as well as Matt Murdock resuming his activities as the vigilante Daredevil, thus both of them returning to using "excessive force."
    • The title of episode 1.07, Art for Art's Sake, is a reference to Muse making paintings and murals through killing people, thus making art "for art's sake."
    • The title of episode 1.08, Isle of Joy, is a reference to the lyrics in the song Manhattan, which played during Fisk's Black & White Ball.
    • The title of episode 1.09, Straight to Hell, is a reference to Mayor Wilson Fisk escalating his Anti-Vigilante Campaign by authorizing a blackout over New York City, breeding chaos and sending it "straight to hell." It is also a reference to Daredevil returning to Hell's Kitchen after quitting one year prior.
    • The title of episode 2.01, The Northern Star, is a reference to the ship of the same name featured within the episode.
    • The title of episode 2.02, Shoot the Moon, is a reference to a quote by Connor Powell about wanting to capture Matt Murdock and Daredevil.
    • The title of episode 2.03, The Scales & The Sword, is a reference to the scales of justice being rigged against Swordsman during his trial, as well as his alias and weapon of choice.
    • The title of episode 2.04, Gloves Off, is a reference to the boxing match between Wilson Fisk and Matterhorn, as well as the events of the episode leading the former to "take his gloves off" and return to his ruthless ways.
    • The title of episode 2.05, The Grand Design, is a reference to the "grand design" that led to Daredevil rescuing Bullseye after trying to kill him before, and the "grand design" that led to Vanessa Fisk meeting Wilson Fisk.
    • The title of episode 2.06, Requiem, is a reference to Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart's Requiem playing during Vanessa Fisk's funeral, as well as the conflict New York City escalating in wake of her death.
    • The title of episode 2.07, The Hateful Darkness, is a reference to Daredevil expressing his hatred to Bullseye for all the people he killed, and part of him wanting to kill him for that.
    • The title of episode 2.08, The Southern Cross, is a reference to the constellation of the same name, contrasting the title of the season's first episode, The Northern Star. The southern cross may also be a reference to Matt Murdock's life turning upside down after the events of the episode.
