    Ironheart trivia

    Background details, easter eggs and behind-the-scenes facts fans have spotted in Ironheart.

    Spotted on screen

    • MIT Riri's student ID is 30216RW, matching March 2, 2016, the date of her first comic appearance. Reported by buzzfeed.com
    • MIT lab A whiteboard reads Griot Algorithm, Shuri's AI from the Black Panther films. Reported by buzzfeed.com
    • Riri takes her suit from MIT MIT's AI assistant Trevor is an animated pencil, likely a nod to Clippy. Reported by buzzfeed.com
    • Riri's home Riri drinks from a Stan's Donuts mug. Reported by buzzfeed.com
    • Riri's car Riri's 1972 Plymouth Barracuda is the car Okoye drove in Wakanda Forever, and its parts end up in her suit. Reported by buzzfeed.com
    • Episode 3, Joe's house Riri finds a bag of ashes labeled Obadiah Stane, revealing Joe is Ezekiel Stane. Reported by buzzfeed.com
    • Episode 2 ending Posters for Faust, one with devil horns, are behind Riri, hinting at Mephisto. Reported by buzzfeed.com
    • Riri crashes Riri crashes into the sign outside White Castle and the leftover letters spell OUCH! Reported by buzzfeed.com
    • Finale When Mephisto stirs his coffee, the spoon reflects his devil form. Reported by buzzfeed.com
