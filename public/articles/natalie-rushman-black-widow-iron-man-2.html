    Who Is Natalie Rushman in Iron Man 2?

    By Alex Continuity Nov 22, 2026 3 min read

    The Answer: Natalie Rushman is a cover name. She is Natasha Romanoff, a S.H.I.E.L.D. agent sent to watch Tony Stark, posing as a Stark Industries employee and then as his new assistant. Iron Man 2 (2010) is the first time Black Widow appears in the MCU, played by Scarlett Johansson.

    Watch the trailer for Iron Man 2

    The Scene

    Natalie arrives at Stark Industries as a paperwork person from the legal department. Tony is sparring with Happy Hogan in his private boxing ring when she walks in with documents for Pepper Potts to sign. Tony invites her into the ring, and in a few seconds she drops Happy to the mat with a move that wraps her legs around his neck. Tony is impressed. Pepper is not.

    Tony looks her up on a screen while she waits. Her file says she speaks several languages and has a modeling past. He decides on the spot that she should be his new assistant, because Pepper has just become chief executive of Stark Industries and needs to hand that job to someone.

    Where to look: Iron Man 2 (2010), the boxing ring scene at Stark's company offices, then the Stark Expo and the Monaco trip, where Natalie stays close to Tony and Pepper.

    The Clues Before the Reveal

    The film plays fair. Natalie is never surprised by anything Tony does. She already knows how to handle him, and she watches him the way a guard watches a door. She also stays close to Tony and Pepper on the trip to Monaco, always within reach.

    The big tell comes after Tony's drunken birthday party, when he is falling apart from palladium poisoning. Nick Fury meets him at a donut shop, and Natalie is there too. Fury explains that she is his agent, Natasha Romanoff, placed at Stark Industries to keep an eye on Tony while S.H.I.E.L.D. decides whether he belongs in the Avengers Initiative.

    The Payoff

    In the last act Natasha drops the assistant act completely. She and Happy drive to Hammer Industries, where Vanko is controlling Justin Hammer's drone suits. Happy struggles with one guard while Natasha takes down the rest of the hallway on her own, then gets into the computer room and hands control of James Rhodes's armor back to him. It is the first look at the fighting style that defines her in every later film.

    The name game also sets up her whole character. Natasha Romanoff spends the MCU slipping in and out of identities, and her past as a trained spy is the core of her solo film, Black Widow (2021). Natalie Rushman is the first mask we see her wear.

    Why It Matters

    Iron Man 2 had a lot to do: a sequel to Tony's story and the first real push toward a shared team film. Natalie is how the movie does the second job without stopping the first. She is a new character with her own secret, and the reveal tells the audience that S.H.I.E.L.D. has been watching Tony since the end of the first film, when Fury showed up in his living room.

    If you rewatch, follow her eyes in the boxing ring and in Monaco. She is never the assistant she claims to be. She is always working.
