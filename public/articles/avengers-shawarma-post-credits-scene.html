    The Avengers Shawarma Scene: When and Why It Was Filmed

    By Ross Props Nov 22, 2026 3 min read

    The Answer: The shawarma scene, the second scene after the credits of The Avengers (2012), was filmed on 12 April 2012, the day after the film's world premiere at the El Capitan Theatre in Los Angeles. Robert Downey Jr. pushed for more after his shawarma line, and Joss Whedon wrote the silent meal into a last-minute shoot.

    Watch the trailer for The Avengers

    The Setup in the Film

    After the battle of New York, Tony Stark falls from the sky out of the portal and lands unconscious. When he wakes up with the team standing over him, his first question is whether anyone has ever tried shawarma, because there is a place a couple of blocks away. It is a throwaway joke in the middle of a ruined city, and the film moves on.

    Then, at the very end of the credits, the joke pays off. The six Avengers sit around a table in a wrecked shawarma restaurant, still in costume, eating in exhausted silence. No one says anything. That is the whole scene, and it became one of the most loved moments of the film.

    Where to look: The Avengers (2012), the second scene after the end credits. The first scene, earlier in the credits, is the Thanos tease: the alien who gave Loki his army tells his master that attacking Earth means courting death, and Thanos turns and smiles.

    Why It Was Shot After the Premiere

    The scene did not exist when the film premiered at the El Capitan Theatre on 11 April 2012. Reports from the time say Downey wanted the shawarma line to lead somewhere, and Whedon turned one line into pages of possible material. The cast was in Los Angeles for the press tour, so the next day, 12 April, they filmed it at a small restaurant on West Pico Boulevard, even though the scene is set in New York. It was ready in time for the film's wide release in May.

    Why Captain America Covers His Face

    Watch Steve Rogers. He leans on his hand and barely eats. Chris Evans was filming Snowpiercer by then and had a full beard for that role. To hide it, he wore a prosthetic over his lower face, which kept him from moving his jaw much, and he kept a hand in front of it for most of the shot. Once you know, it is all you can see.

    Why It Matters

    The Avengers set up the idea that a Marvel film has something waiting after the credits, and the two scenes here show the two jobs those scenes can do. The Thanos tease points to a story years away. The shawarma meal is just a reward for staying in your seat. Marvel has done both kinds ever since.
