    Discussion rules

    1. Be civil. Argue with the idea, not the person. No insults, threats or slurs.
    2. Mark spoilers. Start your post with "Spoiler:" when it gives away a recent movie or show.
    3. Name your source. Rumors and leaks are fine on Coming Soon and Rumors if you say where you read them and call them rumors.
    4. Nothing made up about real people. Actors, directors and crew are real people. No invented quotes or claims about their private lives.
    5. No spam or selling. At most two links a post. A new account's first links wait for a quick check.
    6. No pirated links. Do not post links to leaked footage or illegal streams.
    7. 13 or older. You need to be 13 or older to make an account.

    Hide, Block and Report

    Every post has a big Hide button (hides that post for you) and a Block button (hides everything that person posts, for you only). When enough readers hide or report a post, it moves for everyone into a "Hidden" section at the end, with a note saying how many readers hid it. Report sends a post to our moderators, who can remove it or block the account.
