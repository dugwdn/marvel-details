    About MCU Easter Eggs

    MCU Easter Eggs is dedicated to finding, analyzing, and explaining the hidden details, easter eggs, and continuity threads woven throughout Marvel Cinematic Universe films.

    We believe the best films are those that reward close attention. Our mission is to highlight the craft of filmmaking—from subtle character development to visual storytelling elements—that make Marvel movies work as both standalone stories and part of a larger narrative universe.

    Our Approach

    Each article focuses on a single, specific detail rather than providing a list. We dig into the evidence: where the detail appears, what it means for the character or story, and how it connects to other films in the MCU.

    We are transparent about our methods: Articles are AI-assisted and fact-checked against published sources, interviews, and film content. Visual claims are sourced from production notes, interviews, or confirmed by available materials. When we can't confirm something, we say so explicitly.

    Our Writers

    We have a team of four house writers, each bringing a specialized perspective to Marvel analysis:

    Alex Continuity

    Focus: Character arcs, timelines, and continuity across films. Alex catches the small character moments and internal contradictions that define MCU storytelling.

    Maya Dialogue

    Focus: Dialogue patterns, character voice, and verbal callbacks. Maya uses dialogue data and linguistic analysis to find hidden character threads.

    Ross Props

    Focus: Visual details, props, costume design, and production craft. Ross explores the physical filmmaking that brings stories to life.

    Casey Deleted Scenes

    Focus: Cut footage, deleted scenes, and what ends up on the cutting room floor. Casey bridges theatrical releases with extended editions and special features.

    Jamie Theory

    Focus: Fan theories, speculation, and community discoveries. Jamie explores the "what if" details and connections fans have found.

    Our Commitment to Accuracy

    We take accuracy seriously. Every claim is either sourced from:

    • Interviews with filmmakers and cast
    • Production notes and behind-the-scenes materials
    • Official Marvel releases and statements
    • Visual confirmation from the films themselves

    When we present unconfirmed theories or speculation, we clearly label them as such.

    Disclaimer

    MCU Easter Eggs is not affiliated with Marvel Studios, Disney, or any official Marvel entity. We are independent fans and creators analyzing publicly available films and official materials.

    All article content is AI-assisted and fact-checked. Our writers are house personas designed to bring consistency and voice to the site.

    What We Don't Do

    In respect of copyright and fair use, we do not:

    • Publish full film scripts or complete dialogue transcripts
    • Host film stills or Marvel trademarked content
    • Reproduce entire scenes or footage

    Instead, we use short quotes within critical analysis (fair use), cite our sources, and link to official content where possible.

    How We Find Hidden Details

    Our details come from:

    • Close analysis of film dialogue and visual storytelling
    • Published interviews with directors, writers, and producers
    • Production design notes and behind-the-scenes materials
    • Community discoveries and reader submissions (with credit)
    • Deleted scenes available on home media releases
