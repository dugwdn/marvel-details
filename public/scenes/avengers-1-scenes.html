    The Avengers (2012) - Deleted Scenes

    • Iron ManRobert Downey Jr.
    • Captain AmericaChris Evans
    • ThorChris Hemsworth
    • HulkMark Ruffalo
    • Black WidowScarlett Johansson
    • HawkeyeJeremy Renner

    Release: May 4, 2012 | Director: Joss Whedon | Studio: Marvel Studios / Disney

    The deleted and extended scenes released on The Avengers Blu-ray, including the Maria Hill alternate opening and ending, with the source we checked for each one.

    Loading scenes...
    • Alternate Opening - Maria Hill Interrogation (2:33): The film was to open on the wrecked streets of New York after the battle, then cut to Maria Hill being questioned by an unseen interviewer about how the Tesseract crisis was handled and whether Nick Fury made the right call. This would have framed the whole film as Hill's account of events, told after the fact. Source: SciFiNow: Avengers Assemble deleted scenes review.
    • Extended Scene - Loki and Barton Strategize (1:43): A longer version of the Loki, Selvig and Barton scene. In the added part, Barton tells Loki that Fury will be putting a team together, and Loki says he intends to test them. SciFiNow notes it spells out Loki's plan to let himself be captured, which the finished film leaves to the audience. Source: SciFiNow: Avengers Assemble deleted scenes review.
    • Steve Rogers - A Man Out of Time (3:03): Steve Rogers sits alone going through files on his old comrades, almost all marked deceased apart from Peggy Carter, then looks at files on Howard and Tony Stark. Later he sketches at a cafe, where a waitress offers free wireless and he asks if she means the radio. The scene includes a different Stan Lee cameo from the one in the finished film. Source: SciFiNow: Avengers Assemble deleted scenes review.
    • Nick Fury & World Security Council (0:52): The World Security Council pushes back on Fury's plan to assemble the team, and Fury points out that the brainwashed Barton chose not to kill him, suggesting he can be brought back. Most of what it states is already implied in the finished film. Source: SciFiNow: Avengers Assemble deleted scenes review.
    • Extended Viaduct Fight - Raw Footage (1:40): Unfinished footage from the viaduct fight in which Hawkeye and Black Widow battle attackers, with rough temporary effects for Iron Man. This is raw footage, not a finished scene. Source: SciFiNow: Avengers Assemble deleted scenes review.
    • Fury & Hill Discuss the World Security Council (0:42): On the Helicarrier, Fury confronts Hill for telling the Council about Barton, and she asks whether he has told them who is on his response team. It fits with the alternate opening's framing, which gives Maria Hill a much bigger role. Source: SciFiNow: Avengers Assemble deleted scenes review.
    • Extended Scene - Banner and Security Guard (1:37): After landing in the warehouse, Bruce Banner talks longer with the security guard played by Harry Dean Stanton, who ends up handing him the keys to his motorbike. The extra lines explain where Banner's motorbike in the final battle came from. Source: SciFiNow: Avengers Assemble deleted scenes review.
    • Alternate Ending - Maria Hill Interrogation (2:45): The film returns to Maria Hill's interrogation. She gives her official verdict on the team over the closing scenes, including Thor and Loki leaving for Asgard. SciFiNow suggests it was dropped because it ends the film on a downbeat interview instead of the heroes. Source: SciFiNow: Avengers Assemble deleted scenes review.
