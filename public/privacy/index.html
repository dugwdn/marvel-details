Privacy

Last updated October 6, 2026.

Without an account

The movies you mark as seen, your saved list, your favorite characters, the hidden details you've found and your spoiler setting are kept in your own browser (its "local storage") on this device. They are not sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data, or the "Clear my list on this device" button on My Marvel, removes them.

With an account (optional)

You can sign in with Google so your list follows you to other devices. You must be 13 or older. When you sign in we keep:

  • your Google account number (an ID Google gives us that doesn't reveal who you are on its own),
  • your first name, to say hello on My Marvel,
  • your list (the items above), up to 16 KB,
  • a sign-in session: a random code in a cookie on your device, kept for 30 days or until you sign out.

We never keep your email address, photo or full name. The data is stored in a Cloudflare D1 database for this site. We don't sell it or share it.

Delete it any time: on My Marvel, choose "Delete account". That removes your account, its sessions and the saved copy of your list.

Ads, analytics and embedded content

This site shows Google AdSense ads and may use Google Analytics. Google can use cookies to show and measure ads; see how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Trailers play in YouTube's privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com). Fonts load from Google Fonts. Your member list is not shared with any of these.
