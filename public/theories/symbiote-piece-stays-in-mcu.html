    Still open

    The symbiote scrap left in No Way Home will make a new Venom

    When Eddie Brock was sent home, a small piece of the symbiote stayed behind on a bar counter in the MCU.

    Why fans can't let it go

    Eddie Brock went home. A tiny black drop of him didn't, and it was still moving.

    What fans think

    No Way Home's credits scene ends with Strange's spell pulling Eddie Brock back to his world, but a black drop of symbiote is left on the bar, and it moves. Fans expect it to bond with someone in the MCU and bring the black suit or a new Venom to Tom Holland's Spider-Man; Ned, Harry Osborn or a new character are popular guesses. Tom Holland has hinted the symbiote story is still in Peter's future, but nothing has been announced.

    The evidence board

    1. A piece of the symbiote stays on the bar and moves after Eddie vanishes.

      Spider-Man: No Way Home, mid-credits scene

    2. The piece is read as a setup for a classic black-suit story in the MCU.

      Spider-Man: No Way Home, mid-credits scene

    3. Tom Holland hinted the symbiote story is still to come.

      Interviews after No Way Home

    The case against

    • Sony's Venom films ended Eddie's story in his own world, and Marvel has not said the piece will matter.

    Still open

    Not picked up on screen so far; Spider-Man: Brand New Day's plot does not mention it.
