    Still open

    Loki is the key to Secret Wars

    As the God of Stories holding the multiverse together, Loki is what Doom needs or must stop.

    What fans think

    Loki season 2 ends with Loki on a throne at the end of time, holding the branching timelines together like the world tree Yggdrasil. Fans think that makes him central to Doomsday and Secret Wars: Doom might capture him to control every timeline, or Loki plays the Beyonder role from the original comic. Another theory has Thor and Deadpool going rogue to save him. Tom Hiddleston is in the Doomsday cast.

    The evidence board

    1. Loki becomes the being holding the timelines together as a tree.

      Loki season 2 finale

    2. Hiddleston is in the Doomsday cast.

      Avengers: Doomsday casting

    The case against

    • The God of Stories title comes from a comic that ended just before Secret Wars, so there is little comic guide for it.

    Still open

    Hiddleston returns in Doomsday; Loki's role there has not been shown.

    Where it started

    Started on Reddit and fan sites in 2023. Covered by /Film, FandomWire and Den of Geek.
