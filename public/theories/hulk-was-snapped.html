    Debunked

    The Hulk was dusted in the snap, not Bruce

    A Reddit post said Bruce's head drop after the snap meant the Hulk inside him had been wiped out.

    Why fans can't let it go

    Watch Bruce right after the snap: his head drops. One fan said that was the Hulk dying inside him.

    What fans think

    At the end of Infinity War, as the survivors gather around Vision's body, Bruce drops his head. Reddit user angrydanmarin argued that was the moment the Hulk persona was snapped away, which would make the strongest Avenger a quiet victim of Thanos. BGR covered it but doubted it; Inverse called it 'unequivocally false' and said Bruce was just grieving. Endgame shows Bruce and the Hulk together as one, so the Hulk was never gone.

    The evidence board

    1. Bruce's head drops right after the snap.

      Avengers: Infinity War, the survivors around Vision's body

    2. The Hulk refused to come out through the whole film, which fans tied to the theory.

      Avengers: Infinity War, Wakanda battle

    The case against

    • Almost everyone in that scene reacts with grief, and Endgame brings the Hulk back.

    Debunked

    Endgame has Bruce combine his mind with the Hulk's strength, so the Hulk was not erased.

    Do you buy it?

    One tap. No account needed.

    Where it started

    Started on Reddit in 2019-02 (u/angrydanmarin). Covered by BGR, Inverse, Inside the Magic and ComicBook.com.
