    Debunked

    Maria Hill isn't really dead

    Secret Invasion killed Maria Hill in episode 1, and fans hoped the body was a Skrull.

    Why fans can't let it go

    In a show where anyone could be a shape-shifter, fans hoped Maria Hill's body was a fake. Her face stayed the same.

    What fans think

    Secret Invasion's first episode ends with Maria Hill shot by a Skrull wearing Fury's face. Because the show is about shape-shifters, fans hoped the dead woman was a Skrull or that Hill would turn up later. But her body stays human, unlike dead Skrulls in the series, and Fury brings it home to her mother in episode 2. Director Ali Selim said Hill is dead, while adding that in the MCU anything can happen.

    The evidence board

    1. She is killed by an impostor wearing Fury's face, in a show full of disguises.

      Secret Invasion, episode 1

    2. Skrulls had already played Hill once before, in Far From Home.

      Spider-Man: Far From Home, post-credits scene

    The case against

    • Dead Skrulls in the show change back to their real form; Hill's body stays human and is sent home for burial.

    Debunked

    Her body stays human, and director Ali Selim said she is dead.
