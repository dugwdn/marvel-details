    Wanda survived Mount Wundagore

    The Scarlet Witch didn't die when the temple fell and will return, maybe near Doom.

    Why fans can't let it go

    A mountain fell on Wanda, but nobody saw a body, and that matters in comic book movies.

    What fans think

    Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness ends with Wanda bringing Mount Wundagore down on herself, but we never see a body. Fans have floated America Chavez pulling her out through a portal and pointed to a 'Latverian Witch' costume shown at Comic-Con, echoing comics where Wanda lives in Doom's Latveria. Kevin Feige said Marvel is excited to find out when and how she can return. Elizabeth Olsen has said nothing is agreed.

    The evidence board

    1. Wanda destroys Wundagore and her death is not shown on screen.

      Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness, climax

    2. Kevin Feige said Marvel is 'excited to find out when and how Scarlet Witch can return.'

      Press for Agatha All Along (not a film scene)

    The case against

    • Olsen has said nothing has been agreed for her MCU future.
