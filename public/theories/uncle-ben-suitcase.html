    Confirmed

    Peter's suitcase belonged to Uncle Ben

    The MCU never named Uncle Ben, but Peter packs for Europe with a case marked B.F.P.

    Why fans can't let it go

    Look at the initials on Peter's suitcase as he packs for Europe. The MCU's missing uncle was there all along.

    What fans think

    Fans long asked whether Tom Holland's Peter ever had an Uncle Ben, because the films skipped his origin. In Far From Home, as Peter packs, the suitcase shows the initials B.F.P., and fans read it as Benjamin Franklin Parker. Director Jon Watts confirmed it: 'We have Uncle Ben's suitcase though. Did you notice that?' He said Peter is carrying the baggage of what happened to Ben. Earlier, Watts had said they avoided the subject on purpose.

    The evidence board

    1. The initials on Peter's case are B.F.P.

      Spider-Man: Far From Home, Peter packs for the school trip

    2. Watts said earlier films avoided Ben because they were not telling an origin story.

      Spider-Man: Homecoming, interviews

    3. A trailer breakdown spotted the case before release.

      Spider-Man: Far From Home trailer

    The case against

    • Nobody has said what the F stands for, and Ben has still never been shown or named in an MCU film.

    Confirmed

    Jon Watts told ComicBook.com the case is Uncle Ben's.
