    Still open

    Doom came for baby Franklin's power

    Doctor Doom wants Franklin Richards' reality-bending power for his plans in Doomsday.

    What fans think

    The Fantastic Four: First Steps post-credits scene shows Sue finding Doom, mask in hand and face unseen, beside little Franklin. In the film, Galactus wants Franklin as his successor and Franklin brings Sue back to life, so fans see him as the most powerful being in that world. Theories split: Doom will use him to build something like the comics' Battleworld, Franklin will rebuild Doom's face (letting Downey appear without a mask), or Franklin turns bad.

    The evidence board

    1. Doom sits by Franklin, holding his mask.

      The Fantastic Four: First Steps, post-credits

    2. Galactus wants Franklin as a successor; Franklin revives Sue.

      The Fantastic Four: First Steps, climax

    The case against

    • Some writers think the 'fix his face' version is too small a goal for Doom.

    Still open

    The scene shows no dialogue and no motive; answers wait for Doomsday.
