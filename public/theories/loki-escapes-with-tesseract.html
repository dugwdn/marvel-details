    Confirmed

    Loki isn't gone: a 2012 Loki escapes

    Even after Loki dies in Infinity War, a past Loki lives on through Endgame's time travel.

    Why fans can't let it go

    Thanos killed Loki, yet Marvel had already announced his show. The answer was hiding in 2012.

    What fans think

    Loki is killed by Thanos at the start of Infinity War, yet Marvel had already announced a Loki series. Fans reasoned some Loki had to survive, and many guessed time travel would supply him. In Endgame's 2012 heist, the Tesseract slides to Loki's feet and he vanishes with it. The Loki series follows that 2012 Loki after the TVA arrests him.

    The evidence board

    1. Loki grabs the dropped Tesseract and teleports away.

      Avengers: Endgame, 2012 New York heist

    2. Thanos kills Loki on the Asgardian ship.

      Avengers: Infinity War, opening

    The case against

    • It is a variant, not the Loki who grew over Thor: Ragnarok; the Loki who died stays dead.

    Confirmed

    Endgame shows 2012 Loki escaping with the Tesseract and the Loki series follows him.

    Do you buy it?

    One tap. No account needed.

    Where it started

    We could not find the first post; fans have talked about it since 2018-2019. Covered by TheWrap, Bustle and SYFY's weekly fan-theory roundup.
