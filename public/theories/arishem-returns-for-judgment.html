    Still open

    Arishem is coming back to judge Earth

    The giant Celestial took three Eternals and promised to return to decide whether humanity lives.

    Why fans can't let it go

    A space god the size of a mountain said he'd be back to decide if humans deserve to live. That was 2021.

    What fans think

    At the end of Eternals, the Celestial Arishem appears, takes Sersi, Kingo and Phastos, and says he will come back to judge whether humanity deserves to live. Fans have waited since 2021 for that judgment. Some expect it after Secret Wars, when the MCU turns to mutants, perhaps through a version of the 2022 comic A.X.E.: Judgment Day, where the Celestials judge Earth. No Eternals sequel is announced.

    The evidence board

    1. Arishem promises to come back and judge humanity.

      Eternals, mid-credits Celestial scene

    2. In the comics, Celestials wipe out species that fail their test.

      Eternals, the Celestials' plan

    The case against

    • Marvel has shown no sign of an Eternals sequel, and ComicBook.com worried the mystery may never be resolved.

    Still open

    Arishem has not returned, and no sequel is announced.
