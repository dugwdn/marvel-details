    Partly true

    Loki was under the scepter's spell too

    In The Avengers, the Mind Stone in Loki's scepter was controlling Loki as well as his victims.

    What fans think

    Fans noticed Loki's eyes and behavior in The Avengers seemed off compared with Thor, and that Thanos gave him the scepter. The theory says the Mind Stone was twisting Loki, which would explain his worst acts. Marvel's official character page later added that the scepter was secretly influencing Loki as well. The films never say it out loud.

    The evidence board

    1. Loki uses the scepter to control Hawkeye and Selvig; the scepter came from Thanos's side.

      The Avengers (2012)

    2. Once the scepter is gone, Loki turns friendlier and more playful, which fits the stone having bent his mind before.

      Thor: Ragnarok

    The case against

    • Nothing in the first Avengers film tells us Loki himself was under the stone's influence.

    Partly true

    Marvel's own character bio was updated to say the scepter influenced Loki; no film states it.
