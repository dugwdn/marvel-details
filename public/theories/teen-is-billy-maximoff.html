    Confirmed

    Teen is Wanda's son Billy

    The nameless boy on the Witches' Road had a 'W' over his mouth and blue magic, and fans said he was Billy Maximoff.

    Why fans can't let it go

    A boy with no name, blue magic and a 'W' over his mouth walked the Witches' Road. Fans knew whose son he was.

    What fans think

    Agatha All Along introduced Joe Locke as 'Teen', a boy whose name is magically hidden. Fans noticed the sigil on his mouth looked like a W or M, his magic was blue like Billy's in WandaVision, he said a lot happened to him at 13, and he wanted powers that match the comics' Wiccan. Episode 5 put him in a crown and played Wanda's theme when Agatha said he was like his mother. Episode 6 says his name: Billy Maximoff, reborn in a teenage body.

    The evidence board

    1. A magic mark hides his mouth and name, and fans read its shape as a W or M.

      Agatha All Along, episodes 1 and 2

    2. His crown and blue magic echo the Scarlet Witch, and Wanda's theme plays at 'like your mother'.

      Agatha All Along, episode 5

    3. His costume in the 1980s trial resembles Wiccan's.

      Agatha All Along, episode 5

    The case against

    • The 'like your mother' line also fit the rival guess that he was Agatha's son, Nicholas Scratch.

    Confirmed

    Episode 6 names him as Billy.
