    Confirmed

    Hawkeye's hidden boss is the Kingpin

    The Tracksuit Mafia answered to an 'Uncle' kept out of frame, and fans were sure it was Wilson Fisk.

    Why fans can't let it go

    Hawkeye kept the Tracksuit Mafia's boss just out of frame. A huge shadow and a familiar laugh gave him away.

    What fans think

    In Hawkeye, Maya Lopez and the Tracksuit Mafia work for a man called Uncle, shown only as a huge figure whose face stays out of the shot. Comic fans knew the Kingpin raised Echo after her father's death, and Clint warns Kate about 'the guy at the top'. Episode 5 ends with Yelena sending Kate a photo of Eleanor Bishop with Vincent D'Onofrio's Wilson Fisk in his white comics suit. The finale brings him on screen.

    The evidence board

    1. Maya's benefactor is a big man whose face is hidden and whose laugh sounds familiar.

      Hawkeye, episode 3 flashback to Maya's childhood

    2. In the comics Kingpin raised Maya after her father died, which matches what the show sets up.

      Hawkeye, episodes 2 and 3

    3. Clint tells Kate about a man at the top who will do anything to grow the operation.

      Hawkeye, episode 3

    The case against

    • Until episode 5 the show never named him, and some fans guessed other bosses before the photo.

    Confirmed

    Episode 5 reveals Fisk in a photo, and the finale shows him.

    Do you buy it?

    One tap. No account needed.

    Where it started

    Started on Reddit, X in 2021-12. Covered by Tom's Guide, A.V. Club, TheWrap, SYFY and The Ringer.
