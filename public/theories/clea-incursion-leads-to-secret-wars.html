    Clea's incursion starts the road to Secret Wars

    Clea pulled Strange away to stop an incursion he caused; fans see it as the first domino toward universes colliding.

    Why fans can't let it go

    Clea told Doctor Strange he broke something between universes. Comic fans know where that road ends.

    What fans think

    Multiverse of Madness's credits scene has Clea, from the Dark Dimension, tell Strange he caused an incursion and must help fix it. In the comics, incursions are universes crashing into each other, and they lead straight to Secret Wars. Fans connected Strange's Darkhold dreamwalking, the Illuminati's warning about incursions, and Clea's scene into one chain that ends with Avengers: Secret Wars. Game Informer argued Clea's introduction is a real link to that story.

    The evidence board

    1. Clea tells Strange he caused an incursion and pulls him into the Dark Dimension.

      Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness, mid-credits scene

    2. The Illuminati explain that dreamwalking with the Darkhold sets off incursions.

      Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness, Earth-838

    The case against

    • SYFY noted Clea might be leading Strange into a separate Dark Dimension story, not a Secret Wars one.

    Still open

    Strange and Clea's incursion story has not been followed up on screen yet.

    Do you buy it?

    One tap. No account needed.

    Where it started

    Started on Reddit and fan forums in 2022-05. Covered by Game Informer, SYFY, Den of Geek and BGR.
