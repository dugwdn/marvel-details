    Confirmed

    The kid in the Iron Man mask was Peter Parker

    The boy Iron Man saves at the Stark Expo in Iron Man 2 is a young Peter Parker.

    What fans think

    Near the end of Iron Man 2, a small boy in a plastic Iron Man mask stands up to one of Hammer's drones, and Tony saves him with a quick 'nice work, kid.' Fans noticed the Expo is in Queens and the boy's age roughly fits Peter, so they argued it was Spider-Man's first MCU moment. In June 2017, before Spider-Man: Homecoming, Tom Holland said the kid is Peter. Homecoming director Jon Watts later said the idea was his and that he pitched it to Kevin Feige.

    The evidence board

    1. A boy in an Iron Man mask faces a drone and Tony thanks him.

      Iron Man 2, the Stark Expo battle near the end of the film

    2. The Expo is held in Queens, Peter's home borough, and the boy's age lines up.

      Iron Man 2, Stark Expo

    The case against

    Confirmed

    Tom Holland confirmed it in a 2017 interview; Feige's own sign-off is reported less firmly.
