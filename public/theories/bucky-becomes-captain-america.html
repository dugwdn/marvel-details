    Debunked

    Bucky will be the next Captain America

    When Steve retires, his best friend Bucky takes the shield, like in the comics.

    What fans think

    In the comics Bucky took over as Captain America after Steve's death, so many fans expected the same in Endgame. Instead old Steve gives the shield to Sam Wilson while Bucky watches and nods. Sebastian Stan said Bucky becoming Cap 'makes no sense,' and the Russos said Sam deserved it. Sam carries the shield in The Falcon and the Winter Soldier and Captain America: Brave New World.

    The evidence board

    1. Bucky took the role in the comics.

      Comics (not film)

    2. The Endgame writers said Bucky as the next Cap was 'Considered, but quickly dropped.'

      Behind the scenes of Avengers: Endgame (writers' tweet)

    The case against

    • Old Steve hands the shield to Sam on the bench by the lake.

    Debunked

    Steve gives the shield to Sam at the end of Endgame.

    Where it started

    We could not find the first post; fans have talked about it since 2014-2019. Covered by CinemaBlend, NME, Looper and MovieWeb; the cast and directors were asked about it.
