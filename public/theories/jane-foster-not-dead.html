    Jane Foster isn't really gone

    Love and Thunder ends with Jane at Valhalla's gates; a Reddit theory says she survives.

    Why fans can't let it go

    Jane Foster made it to Valhalla, and Heimdall was waiting. In the comics, that was not the end of her story.

    What fans think

    In Thor: Love and Thunder, Jane Foster, fighting cancer, dies after the final battle, and a post-credits scene shows her welcomed to Valhalla by Heimdall. In the comics, Jane dies and is brought back with Mjolnir's power and Odin's magic. A Reddit theory, reported by We Got This Covered, says small moments in earlier Asgard stories show she can return.

    The evidence board

    1. Jane arrives in Valhalla and is greeted by Heimdall.

      Thor: Love and Thunder, post-credits scene

    2. In the comics Jane is brought back to life after dying from cancer.

      Thor: Love and Thunder, Jane's story

    The case against

    • The film treats her death as an ending, and Natalie Portman has not been announced for anything since.

    Still open

    On screen she has died and reached Valhalla; no return is announced.

    Do you buy it?

    One tap. No account needed.

    Where it started

    Started on Reddit in 2022. Covered by We Got This Covered, Looper, Gizmodo, Bustle and The Digital Fix.
