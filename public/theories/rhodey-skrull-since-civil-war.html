    Confirmed

    Rhodey has been a Skrull for years

    Secret Invasion revealed Rhodey was a Skrull; fans then dug for when the real one was replaced, and the director said Civil War.

    Why fans can't let it go

    Rhodey called Fury 'Nick' once, and fans heard the mistake. Then the real Rhodey turned up in a hospital gown.

    What fans think

    Secret Invasion's episode 4 shows that James Rhodes is a Skrull impostor. Fans had already noticed him calling Fury 'Nick' instead of 'Fury' when firing him, a slip they read as Fury realizing the truth. Most first assumed he was swapped after Endgame, but the finale shows the real Rhodey in a hospital gown, struggling to walk, which fans tied to his paralysis in Captain America: Civil War. Director Ali Selim answered 'Yep' when asked whether Rhodey was replaced back then.

    The evidence board

    1. Rhodey calls Fury 'Nick' just before firing him, which sounds wrong for him.

      Secret Invasion, Rhodey's meeting where he fires Fury

    2. The rescued Rhodey wears a hospital gown and can barely walk.

      Secret Invasion, episode 6

    The case against

    • Selim also said it would take 'a librarian' to sort out every Rhodey scene, and Marvel has not given an official timeline.
