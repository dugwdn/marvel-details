    Doom thinks he is saving everyone

    Like Thanos in Infinity War, Doom is the hero of his own story, trying to rescue a dying multiverse.

    Why fans can't let it go

    Infinity War told Thanos's side. Fans think Doomsday will make you understand Doom too.

    What fans think

    Infinity War told much of its story from Thanos's side, and fans expect the Russos to do the same with Doom. With the multiverse collapsing, Doom may be gathering power, Franklin included, to save what is left, his own way. The First Steps post-credits scene with Franklin and trailer moments with Wakanda's leaders meeting the Thing feed this reading. Fans connect it to the comic Secret Wars, where Doom rules Battleworld as a god who kept reality alive.

    The evidence board

    1. Doomsday's premise is a collapsing multiverse.

      Avengers: Doomsday premise

    2. Doom examines Franklin.

      The Fantastic Four: First Steps, post-credits

    The case against

    • A Comic-Con trailer shows Doom raising Sentinels against all three teams, which reads more like a conqueror.
