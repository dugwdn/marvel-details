    Still open

    Miles Morales makes his live-action debut in Secret Wars

    Comic fans expect Miles to cross into the MCU when universes collide, as he did in the 2015 Secret Wars.

    Why fans can't let it go

    In the comics, Miles Morales crossed into the main Marvel world when universes collided. Fans think the MCU will copy that.

    What fans think

    In the 2015 Secret Wars comic, Miles Morales survives the end of his universe and joins the main Marvel world. Fans think Avengers: Secret Wars (December 17, 2027) is the natural place for a live-action Miles. They point to Aaron Davis's nephew line in Homecoming, Tom Holland saying he knows when Miles is coming, and a theory from an X user that Miles is pulled into an incursion at the end of Beyond the Spider-Verse (June 18, 2027) and lands on Battleworld. Feige has said Sony asked Marvel to stay away while the animated films run.

    The evidence board

    1. Aaron Davis mentions a nephew in Homecoming.

      Spider-Man: Homecoming, the parking garage

    2. Tom Holland said he knows when Miles will arrive.

      Spider-Man: Brand New Day press

    3. A fan theory on X has Miles pulled through an incursion into Battleworld.

      Beyond the Spider-Verse and Secret Wars

    The case against

    • Sony controls Miles and is still making animated films with him, which may keep him out of live action for now.

    Still open

    Not announced; Secret Wars opens December 17, 2027.
