    Partly true

    Cap could lift Mjolnir all along

    When Mjolnir wobbled for Steve in Age of Ultron, he was already worthy but held back.

    What fans think

    At the Avengers party in Age of Ultron, everyone tries to lift Thor's hammer; when Steve pulls, it budges and Thor looks worried. Fans argued Steve was worthy and either chose not to embarrass Thor or was held back by the secret that Bucky killed Tony's parents. In Endgame Steve lifts Mjolnir and fights Thanos with it. Anthony Russo later said 'He always could,' and that Steve stopped to spare Thor's pride.

    The evidence board

    1. Mjolnir moves slightly when Steve pulls, and Thor looks alarmed.

      Avengers: Age of Ultron, the party at Avengers Tower

    2. Steve catches and swings Mjolnir against Thanos.

      Avengers: Endgame, final battle

    The case against

    • The Russos' answer (he chose not to embarrass Thor) differs from the popular 'his secret about Bucky made him unworthy' reading.

    Partly true

    Endgame shows Steve lifting Mjolnir and the Russos say he always could; the 'secret held him back' version was not their reading.

    Where it started

    We could not find the first post; fans have talked about it since 2015. One of the most debated MCU moments; the Russos answered it in a Reddit AMA; covered by Looper, Fatherly, Flickering Myth.
