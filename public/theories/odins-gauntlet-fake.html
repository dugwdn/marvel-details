    Confirmed

    The gauntlet in Odin's vault is a fake

    The Infinity Gauntlet seen in Asgard's vault in Thor is a copy, not the one Thanos wears.

    What fans think

    Thor (2011) shows an Infinity Gauntlet in Odin's treasure room, but Age of Ultron shows Thanos putting on his own gauntlet. Fans fixed the clash with a theory that Odin's was a fake or a replica, and Reddit users added backstories about Odin once chasing the stones. Thor: Ragnarok settled the basic point when Hela, in the vault, calls it fake. Kevin Feige said, 'We had an internal Marvel theory that it was fake.'

    The evidence board

    1. An Infinity Gauntlet sits in Odin's vault.

      Thor (2011), the weapons vault

    2. Hela looks at the gauntlet and says it is fake.

      Thor: Ragnarok, Hela in the vault

    The case against

    • Why Odin kept a fake is never explained on screen.

    Confirmed

    Hela calls the vault gauntlet fake in Thor: Ragnarok; the Odin backstory versions stay fan ideas.

    Where it started

    Started on Reddit (several threads, per CinemaBlend). Feige and Taika Waititi both addressed it; covered by ScreenCrush, /Film, Inverse, Looper and Gizmodo.
