    Confirmed

    Adamantium came from the dead Celestial

    A giant stone Celestial was left in the ocean after Eternals; fans said it would give the MCU adamantium, Wolverine's metal.

    Why fans can't let it go

    A dead space god was left standing in the ocean. Fans guessed what was inside it, and it leads to Wolverine.

    What fans think

    Eternals ends with the Celestial Tiamut frozen half out of the Indian Ocean. Fans guessed the body would become a source of a rare metal, and as soon as Marvel owned the X-Men rights, adamantium was the obvious pick. Captain America: Brave New World's footage showed President Ross saying adamantium belongs to everyone, at a summit about 'Celestial Island'. Director Julius Onah confirmed it, and said adamantium will be part of Wolverine's story later.

    The evidence board

    1. Tiamut's body is left sticking out of the sea.

      Eternals, the ending

    2. Ross announces that adamantium belongs to everyone.

      Captain America: Brave New World, the Celestial Island summit

    The case against

    • In the comics adamantium is a man-made alloy, not something found in space rock, so this is a big change.

    Confirmed

    Brave New World says adamantium was found in Tiamut's remains; the director tied it to Wolverine.
