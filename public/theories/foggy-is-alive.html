    Debunked

    Foggy Nelson survived Bullseye's shot

    Daredevil: Born Again killed Foggy in its first episode, and fans hoped it was a trick.

    Why fans can't let it go

    Foggy died in the first episode. Then Marvel said his actor was coming back. Charlie Cox had a short answer.

    What fans think

    Born Again opens with Bullseye shooting Foggy Nelson at Josie's Bar. Fans hoped it was faked: maybe Foggy went into hiding or witness protection, maybe a strange glowing stone in a later episode could bring him back. Marvel TV head Brad Winderbaum then said Elden Henson would return in season 2. Charlie Cox shut the theory down in November 2025: 'Daredevil heard his heart stop. He's dead.' He added, 'I love that theory, but it's wrong.'

    The evidence board

    1. Brad Winderbaum said Elden Henson is coming back for season 2.

      Daredevil: Born Again, season 2 news

    2. A glowing orange stone targeted by robbers in episode 5 made some fans think of the Soul Stone and a way back.

      Daredevil: Born Again, episode 5

    The case against

    • The death happens on screen and the showrunner said it had to; Cox said the theory is wrong.

    Debunked

    Charlie Cox said plainly that Foggy is dead; reports say his season 2 return is through flashbacks.

    Do you buy it?

    One tap. No account needed.

    Where it started

    Started on Reddit and X in 2025-03 (Twitter). Covered by ComingSoon, SuperHeroHype, TechRadar, Newsweek and Inverse.
