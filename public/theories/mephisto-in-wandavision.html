    Debunked

    Mephisto is behind WandaVision

    The devil-like Mephisto is the hidden villain pulling Wanda's strings.

    What fans think

    WandaVision's odd commercials and comics stories in which Wanda's twins are tied to Mephisto led fans to expect him to appear. Every week, fans hunted for clues and many were sure he would show up in the finale. He never did. Showrunner Jac Schaeffer said the real villain was grief and Agatha was the outside threat, and that Mephisto was never part of the plan.

    The evidence board

    1. The twins Billy and Tommy are linked to Mephisto in the comics.

      WandaVision episodes 2 to 9

    2. Fans read the show's many devil references and dark, magical mood as hints; the showrunner later called them a coincidence.

      WandaVision, across the season

    The case against

    • Schaeffer: 'The big bad is grief.' She said she didn't know who Mephisto was until press.

    Debunked

    Mephisto never appears, and the showrunner said he was never planned.

    Where it started

    We could not find the first post; fans have talked about it since 2021. So widespread that the showrunner was asked about it in several interviews; covered by SYFY, Looper and ComicBook.com.
