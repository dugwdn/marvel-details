    T'Challa's son will grow up impossibly fast

    Toussaint was six in Wakanda Forever; Black Panther 3 cast an adult, so fans are hunting for how.

    Why fans can't let it go

    T'Challa's son was a little boy in Wakanda Forever. The next Black Panther film cast him as a grown man.

    What fans think

    Wakanda Forever's mid-credits scene introduces Toussaint, T'Challa's young son, whose Wakandan name is T'Challa. At San Diego Comic-Con 2026, Marvel revealed David Jonsson as an adult Toussaint for Black Panther 3. Since a 20-year time jump seems unlikely, fans have theories: he comes from another timeline after Secret Wars, or, as Inverse suggested, a space journey through a time anomaly like the comic 'The Intergalactic Empire of Wakanda' lets him come back grown.

    The evidence board

    1. Toussaint is a young boy living in Haiti with Nakia.

      Black Panther: Wakanda Forever, mid-credits scene

    2. Marvel cast David Jonsson as an adult Toussaint.

      San Diego Comic-Con 2026, Hall H

    3. Fans connect his age jump to the timeline changes expected in Secret Wars.

      Black Panther 3 announcement

    The case against

    • Marvel could simply jump ahead in time, or use comic-style space travel, with no multiverse needed.

    Still open

    Black Panther 3 has been announced; how Toussaint is an adult is unexplained.
