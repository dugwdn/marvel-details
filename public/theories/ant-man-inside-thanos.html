    Debunked

    Ant-Man would shrink inside Thanos and grow

    Ant-Man shrinks, gets inside Thanos, then grows to full size and bursts him apart from within.

    What fans think

    Before Endgame, fans brainstormed the silliest ways to stop Thanos, and the one that stuck was Scott shrinking, slipping inside Thanos, and growing to full size. It went viral on Twitter and Reddit and turned into songs and memes. Paul Rudd said, 'I've been asked about it a bunch.' Endgame writer Christopher Markus said it would fail because Thanos's body is tough enough to crush Scott.

    The evidence board

    1. Scott can shrink and grow at will.

      Ant-Man and Ant-Man and the Wasp

    2. Scott survives the snap and returns from the Quantum Realm, so he is one of the few heroes left to fight.

      Avengers: Endgame teaser trailer

    The case against

    • Markus said Thanos's body resists the Hulk's punches, so Scott would just be crushed.
    • Anthony Russo called it the most ridiculous fan theory they had heard.

    Debunked

    It does not happen in Endgame, and co-writer Christopher Markus explained why it would not work.

    Do you buy it?

    One tap. No account needed.

    Where it started

    Started on Reddit, then Twitter in 2018 (credited to @filmgloss by reports). Viral joke theory; Paul Rudd, the Russos, Markus and Neil deGrasse Tyson were all asked about it; covered by NME, /Film, GameSpot and CinemaBlend.
