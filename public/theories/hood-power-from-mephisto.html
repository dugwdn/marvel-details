    Confirmed

    The Hood's cloak came from Mephisto

    Ironheart's villain wore a magic hood, and fans said Mephisto, not Dormammu, gave it to him.

    Why fans can't let it go

    Fans waited four years for Marvel's devil to appear. He finally came, hiding behind a stolen hood.

    What fans think

    In the comics the Hood's cloak comes from Dormammu, and a character in Ironheart seemed to agree. But fans had waited for Mephisto since WandaVision, and the trailers hinted at a long-awaited villain. A fan theory said Parker Robbins made a deal with the devil, and that the hood carried pain and voices. The finale shows Mephisto, played by Sacha Baron Cohen, giving Parker the hood in a flashback.

    The evidence board

    1. Parker suffers pain and hears voices when he is without the hood, which fans read as a devil's bargain.

      Ironheart, Parker's scenes with the hood

    2. A trailer seemed to tease a villain fans had wanted for years.

      Ironheart trailer

    The case against

    • Zelma tells Riri the hood is Dormammu's, as in the comics, which pointed the other way until the finale.

    Confirmed

    The finale shows Mephisto gave Parker the hood.

    Do you buy it?

    One tap. No account needed.

    Where it started

    Started on Reddit and X in 2025-06 (Twitter). Covered by Den of Geek, Looper, TechRadar, Inverse and Soap Central; Mephisto had been a fan theory since 2021.
