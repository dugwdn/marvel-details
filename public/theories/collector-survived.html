    Confirmed

    The Collector survived Thanos on Knowhere

    Thanos showed the Guardians a burning museum, but it was an illusion, and fans insisted the Collector lived.

    Why fans can't let it go

    Thanos made the Guardians see a burning museum on Knowhere. One line in a Christmas special proves its owner walked away.

    What fans think

    In Infinity War, the Guardians find Knowhere in flames and the Collector begging Thanos, then learn the scene was a Reality Stone illusion. Nobody sees the Collector's body, so fans argued he lived. Benicio del Toro said in 2018, 'I think he's alive.' The Guardians of the Galaxy Holiday Special settled it: Nebula says the team bought Knowhere from the Collector. James Gunn later answered 'Yes' when asked whether he survived.

    The evidence board

    1. Thanos uses the Reality Stone to fake the scene in the Collector's museum, so what happened to him is unclear.

      Avengers: Infinity War, the Guardians arrive at Knowhere

    2. Nebula mentions buying Knowhere from the Collector.

      The Guardians of the Galaxy Holiday Special

    The case against

    • He has not been seen alive on screen since, and del Toro said he is unsure of the character's future.

    Confirmed

    The Holiday Special says the Guardians bought Knowhere from him, and James Gunn confirmed he lived.
