    The Avengers will fight the X-Men

    In Doomsday the Avengers and the old Fox X-Men clash before teaming up against Doom.

    What fans think

    Doomsday's cast includes Patrick Stewart, Ian McKellen, James Marsden, Kelsey Grammer, Channing Tatum, Rebecca Romijn and Alan Cumming, the X-Men of Fox's films. Fans expect an Avengers vs. X-Men fight first. Trailer footage reportedly shows Shang-Chi fighting Gambit and Mystique fighting Yelena, and a Comic-Con 2026 trailer showed Doom sending Sentinels after all three teams.

    The evidence board

    1. The X-Men of Earth-10005 are part of the cast.

      Avengers: Doomsday casting

    2. Shang-Chi reportedly fights Gambit, and Mystique fights Yelena, in a CinemaCon trailer.

      Avengers: Doomsday CinemaCon trailer (April 2026)

    The case against

    • Beast was friendly with Monica Rambeau in The Marvels, and the Sentinels footage shows a shared enemy.

    Still open

    Doomsday is not out yet; how the teams meet is unknown.

    Where it started

    We could not find the first post; fans have talked about it since 2025. Listed by Looper among Doomsday theories that make sense.
