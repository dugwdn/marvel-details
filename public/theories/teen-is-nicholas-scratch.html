    Teen is Agatha's lost son Nicholas

    Agatha was strangely drawn to the nameless boy, and some fans said he was her dead son returned.

    Why fans can't let it go

    Agatha couldn't stop watching the nameless boy. Some fans thought she had found her lost son.

    What fans think

    Before Teen was revealed as Billy Maximoff, a rival theory said he was Nicholas Scratch, Agatha's son in the comics, who died young in the show's backstory. Fans noted that 'you're so much like your mother' could fit either boy. In episode 4 Rio tells Agatha the boy is not hers, and in episode 5 the Ouija board scene reaches Nicholas as a dead child's voice. Episode 6 names Teen as Billy.

    The evidence board

    1. The 'like your mother' line could point to Agatha as much as Wanda.

      Agatha All Along, episode 5

    2. Rio has to tell Agatha outright that the boy is not hers, showing the question was in Agatha's mind too.

      Agatha All Along, episode 4

    The case against

    • The Ouija board scene treats Nicholas as dead and separate from Teen.

    Debunked

    Rio says he isn't Agatha's son, and episode 6 names him Billy.

    Started on Reddit and X in 2024-09 (Twitter). Covered by ComicBook.com, Inverse, Kotaku Australia and Epicstream.
