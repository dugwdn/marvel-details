    Still open

    Steve met Red Skull when he returned the Soul Stone

    Returning the stones meant Steve had to go to Vormir, where his oldest enemy now stands guard.

    Why fans can't let it go

    To return the Soul Stone, Steve had to climb Vormir, and his oldest enemy was waiting at the top.

    What fans think

    Endgame ends with Steve traveling back to return every Infinity Stone, including the Soul Stone on Vormir, whose guardian is Red Skull. The film never shows that visit. Before release, a Reddit post predicted a showdown there; after it, fans asked what the two said to each other. The Russo brothers have joked about it and, in a fan-reported Reddit Q&A, said the meeting happened off screen. A rival theory by u/Doobledump says the stones only had to go back to the right time, not the right place, so Steve could have skipped Vormir.

    The evidence board

    1. Steve leaves to return all six stones to their places in time.

      Avengers: Endgame, the time platform

    2. Red Skull is bound to Vormir as the Stonekeeper.

      Avengers: Infinity War, Vormir

    3. The directors discussed what Steve would have faced to return the Soul Stone.

      Avengers: Endgame, interviews after release

    The case against

    • The stones may only need to return to their moment in time, so Steve may never have set foot on Vormir.

    Still open
