    Debunked

    Kate's mom's fiance Jack is the real villain

    Jack Duquesne was a smiling swordsman with a dead uncle, and the show seemed to point right at him.

    Why fans can't let it go

    He fenced, he smirked, and his rich uncle turned up dead. Hawkeye made Jack look guilty on purpose.

    What fans think

    In Hawkeye, Kate Bishop's mother is engaged to Jack Duquesne, who fences, acts shady and inherits after his uncle Armand is found dead. Comic readers knew Jack as the Swordsman, and fans suspected him of the murder, of the Tracksuit Mafia, even of being a fake Ronin. A writer at The Cosmic Circus called him a red herring. The finale shows Kate's mother Eleanor working with the Kingpin; Jack was innocent.

    The evidence board

    1. Jack is a skilled fencer who seems to benefit from Armand's death.

      Hawkeye, episodes 1 and 2

    2. A Reddit theory argued Jack, not Clint, killed Maya's father while posing as Ronin.

      Hawkeye, episode 3

    The case against

    • Episode 5 shows Eleanor meeting Fisk, and Jack ends the series cleared.

    Debunked

    Eleanor, not Jack, was working with Kingpin.

    Started on Reddit in 2021-11. Covered by Looper, The Cosmic Circus and Inverse.
