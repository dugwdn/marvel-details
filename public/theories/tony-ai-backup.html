    Still open

    Tony left behind an AI copy of himself

    Before dying, Tony uploaded his mind, and his hologram message was that AI talking.

    What fans think

    An r/FanTheories thread argued Tony's goodbye hologram in Endgame was too precise to be a recording: how did he know where Morgan would sit? The theory says Tony built an AI backup of himself, as happens in the comics, where an AI Tony mentors Riri Williams (Ironheart). After Far From Home's trailer, fans suggested the EDITH glasses voice was really Tony, but EDITH turned out to be a separate AI. Downey has since been cast as Doom, so a Tony AI return remains a fan hope.

    The evidence board

    1. Tony's recorded hologram plays for his family after his funeral.

      Avengers: Endgame, after the funeral

    2. Peter inherits Tony's EDITH glasses.

      Spider-Man: Far From Home

    The case against

    • EDITH is her own system, not Tony.

    Still open

    No film has shown a Tony AI; the EDITH version was ruled out when EDITH was its own AI.
