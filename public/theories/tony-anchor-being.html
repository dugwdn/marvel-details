    Still open

    Tony was the anchor of the MCU's universe

    Tony Stark was Earth-616's 'anchor being,' so his death started the universe falling apart.

    Why fans can't let it go

    Deadpool & Wolverine explained anchor beings. Fans think the MCU lost its anchor in Endgame.

    What fans think

    Deadpool & Wolverine explained that some universes have an anchor being whose death slowly kills that world. Fans connected this to Tony: his death in Endgame could be why the multiverse is collapsing, and a Tony-faced Doom might step in as a replacement anchor. Some also tie it to Strange's 14,000,605 futures and Clea's line that Strange caused an incursion. Kevin Feige was asked and did not confirm or deny it.

    The evidence board

    1. The TVA explains anchor beings and how their death unravels a universe.

      Deadpool & Wolverine

    2. Clea tells Strange he caused an incursion.

      Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness, mid-credits

    The case against

    • If every anchor's death dooms its world, every timeline would be bound to self-destruct.

    Still open

    Nothing on screen names the MCU's anchor being; Feige would not say.

    Do you buy it?

    One tap. No account needed.

    Where it started

    We could not find the first post; fans have talked about it since 2024. Covered by Inverse, BGR, Looper and Nerdist; Feige was asked about it in an interview.
