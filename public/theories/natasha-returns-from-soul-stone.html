    Debunked

    Natasha can come back from the Soul Stone

    Black Widow's soul lives inside the Soul Stone and she can be freed or traded back.

    What fans think

    Natasha dies on Vormir so Clint can get the Soul Stone, and Hulk later says he tried to bring her back. One r/FanTheories post argued Hulk had seen her inside the stone, the way Thanos sees young Gamora. Others hoped Steve returning the stone would return her too. The writers ruled it out ('It's not like a pawn shop'), and Scarlett Johansson said, 'Natasha is dead. She is dead. She's dead.'

    The evidence board

    1. Thanos meets young Gamora in an orange dream world after the snap.

      Avengers: Infinity War, after the snap

    2. Hulk tells the team he tried to bring her back.

      Avengers: Endgame, after the snap

    The case against

    • Markus: putting the stone back does not return anything.
    • Johansson closed the door on a return.

    Debunked

    The writers said returning the stone gives nothing back, and Johansson said Natasha is dead.

    Where it started

    Started on r/FanTheories in 2020 (u/Crispworthy, per Inverse). The original post. Covered by Inverse, Looper, TheWrap and Newsweek.
