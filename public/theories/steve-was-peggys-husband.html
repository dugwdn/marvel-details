    Partly true

    Steve was Peggy's mystery husband

    The husband Peggy mentions in The Winter Soldier was Steve Rogers, after he went back in time.

    What fans think

    In The Winter Soldier, Peggy mentions a husband without naming him. After Endgame sent Steve back to the past to live with Peggy, fans argued he had been that husband all along, and some even pointed to an older pallbearer at Peggy's funeral in Civil War as old Steve. Endgame writers Christopher Markus and Stephen McFeely have said they believe this. The directors, the Russo brothers, say Steve's trip made a separate branch timeline.

    The evidence board

    1. Peggy mentions an unnamed husband.

      Captain America: The Winter Soldier

    2. Steve returns as an old man after living a life with Peggy.

      Avengers: Endgame, final scenes

    3. An older man among Peggy's pallbearers could be old Steve; Markus said he likes that idea.

      Captain America: Civil War, Peggy's funeral

    The case against

    • The Russos say Steve's trip created a new timeline where Peggy had not yet met her husband.
    • Critics say Peggy's reactions to Steve in The Winter Soldier don't fit a long marriage to him.

    Partly true

    The writers back the theory; the directors say Steve lived in a different timeline, so Marvel has not settled it.

    Where it started

    We could not find the first post; fans have talked about it since 2019. Writers and directors publicly disagreed about it; covered by The Hollywood Reporter, CinemaBlend, Elite Daily and SYFY.
