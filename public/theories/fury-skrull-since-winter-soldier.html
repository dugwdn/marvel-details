    The Nick Fury we've watched since 2014 is a Skrull

    A joke Reddit post about toast grew into a theory that Talos replaced Fury after The Winter Soldier.

    Why fans can't let it go

    Young Fury swears he can't eat diagonally cut toast. Watch what he eats at the Barton farm in Age of Ultron.

    What fans think

    In Captain Marvel, young Fury says he can't eat toast cut diagonally, a detail to prove he is not a Skrull. A Redditor noticed Fury eating a diagonally cut sandwich in Age of Ultron and suggested the Fury in later films was an impostor; another version says the real Fury was taken to space to heal after nearly dying in The Winter Soldier, with Talos filling in. Far From Home showed Talos really was posing as Fury at least once. The Captain Marvel directors joked Fury may just have changed his mind about toast.

    The evidence board

    1. Fury says he can't eat diagonally cut toast.

      Captain Marvel, Fury and Carol test each other

    2. Fury eats a diagonally cut sandwich at the Barton farm.

      Avengers: Age of Ultron, Barton farmhouse

    3. Talos and Soren posed as Fury and Maria Hill through Far From Home.

      Spider-Man: Far From Home, post-credits scene

    The case against

    • The poster admitted the bread did not look toasted and filed the post under humor.

    Still open
