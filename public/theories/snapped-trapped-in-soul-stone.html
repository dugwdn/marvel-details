    Debunked

    The snapped heroes are alive inside the Soul Stone

    Everyone dusted by Thanos is trapped in the Soul Stone, waiting to be let out.

    What fans think

    After the snap, Thanos visits an orange dreamlike place and talks to young Gamora, the same color as the Soul Stone. Fans reasoned that all snap victims went there too, as in the Infinity Gauntlet comics, so Endgame would free them from the stone. Instead the Avengers use time travel to get the stones and Hulk snaps everyone back, with no 'Soul World' rescue.

    The evidence board

    1. Thanos talks to young Gamora in an orange space.

      Avengers: Infinity War, after the snap

    2. Joe Russo confirmed the young Gamora scene happens inside the Soul Stone, which fans stretched to cover everyone who was snapped.

      Avengers: Infinity War, after the snap

    The case against

    • Time travel and a second snap, not a Soul World, undo the losses.

    Debunked

    Endgame brings the victims back with a new snap, not from inside the Soul Stone.

    Do you buy it?

    One tap. No account needed.

    Where it started

    We could not find the first post; fans have talked about it since 2018. Listed in GamesRadar, Stanford Daily and SYFY Endgame theory roundups; WatchMojo counts it among top wrong ones.
