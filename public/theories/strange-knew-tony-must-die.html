    Partly true

    Strange's one winning future needed Tony to die

    Doctor Strange saw that the only future where they win is the one where Tony sacrifices himself.

    Why fans can't let it go

    Strange saw 14,000,605 futures and won in one. Fans think Tony's death was the price from the start.

    What fans think

    In Infinity War, Strange looks at 14,000,605 futures and finds one win, then hands Thanos the Time Stone to save Tony. Fans argued Strange chose Tony because Tony's death was part of that single win. In Endgame's final battle, Strange raises one finger to Tony, Tony understands, and snaps. A popular r/FanTheories post went further and argued Tony also had to die because he caused trouble in futures after Thanos.

    The evidence board

    1. Strange holds up one finger to Tony just before the snap (Benedict Cumberbatch improvised it).

      Avengers: Endgame, final battle

    2. Strange trades the Time Stone for Tony's life on Titan.

      Avengers: Infinity War, Titan

    The case against

    • The Russos only confirmed Strange lived each future; they never said Tony's death was required in every version of the win.

    Partly true

    Joe Russo said Tony knows the solution means snapping and dying; the extra 'Tony caused later trouble' version is not confirmed.

    Do you buy it?

    One tap. No account needed.

    Where it started

    Started on r/FanTheories in 2021. The original post. Covered by Inverse, Looper and BGR; the 'one finger' moment is one of Endgame's most talked-about beats.
