    Still open

    Steve comes back as Nomad

    Steve Rogers returns in Doomsday not as Captain America but as Nomad.

    What fans think

    Steve retired in Endgame and Sam now carries the shield, yet Doomsday's teaser said 'Steve Rogers will return.' Fans noticed it didn't say Captain America, and in the comics Steve became Nomad when he gave up the role. Anthony Mackie pointed to the comics but said he didn't know. Chris Evans said Marvel pitched him Nomad films after Endgame and he turned them down; other fans think this is an old Steve, a variant or Hydra Cap.

    The evidence board

    1. A teaser shown before Avatar: Fire and Ash revealed Evans as Steve.

      Avengers: Doomsday teaser

    2. The teaser says 'Steve Rogers will return,' not 'Captain America'.

      Avengers: Doomsday teaser card

    The case against

    • Evans turned down Nomad pitches before, so Nomad is not a given.

    Still open

    Evans is confirmed to return; which Steve and under what name is unknown.
