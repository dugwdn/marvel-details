    Doctor Doom is a Tony Stark from another world

    Robert Downey Jr.'s Doom is a dark Tony Stark variant from another universe.

    Why fans can't let it go

    Iron Man's actor is playing Marvel's biggest villain. Fans think the face is not a coincidence.

    What fans think

    When Downey was announced as Victor von Doom for Avengers: Doomsday, fans asked how Iron Man's face could end up on Marvel's biggest villain. The leading answer is that Doom is a Tony from another reality who went down a darker road, following MCU multiverse ideas from Loki and No Way Home. Fans also cite Thanos calling Tony 'cursed with knowledge' and comics where Tony and Doom swap roles. Downey has said he is playing Victor von Doom, but how the two faces connect is still unexplained.

    The evidence board

    1. Downey, who played Tony Stark, is cast as Victor von Doom.

      Avengers: Doomsday casting

    2. Thanos says Tony is 'cursed with knowledge'.

      Avengers: Infinity War, Titan

    The case against

    • Den of Geek argues Doom works better as a dark mirror of Tony than as a literal variant.

    Still open

    Doomsday opens December 18, 2026; Marvel has not explained why Doom looks like Tony.

    We could not find the first post; fans have talked about it since 2024. Looper calls it the theory most likely to come true; covered by Looper, ScreenCrush, Tom's Guide, Den of Geek.
