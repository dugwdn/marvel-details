    Still open

    The handshake broke Strange's spell on Ned

    Ned remembers Peter at the end of Brand New Day because their secret handshake undid the magic.

    What fans think

    Brand New Day ends with Peter meeting Ned at a coffee shop and starting their old secret handshake; Ned joins in and says Peter's name. Fans point out Ned used a sling ring and showed magic talent in No Way Home, so the handshake may carry a spark that cracks the spell. Others think it is only muscle memory. Jacob Batalon asked, 'What if it's just muscle memory?' and called it 'really up to interpretation for everyone.'

    The evidence board

    1. Ned completes the handshake and says Peter's name.

      Spider-Man: Brand New Day, final scene

    2. Ned opens portals with a sling ring.

      Spider-Man: No Way Home

    The case against

    • Batalon suggests it might only be muscle memory.

    Still open

    The film shows Ned saying Peter's name but does not explain how, and Batalon calls it open.

    Do you buy it?

    We could not find the first post; fans have talked about it since 2026. Covered by SYFY, TheWrap, Den of Geek, Kotaku and IBTimes within weeks of release.
