    Debunked

    Doctor Strange shows up in WandaVision

    Wanda was set for the next Doctor Strange film, so fans expected him to step into Westview.

    Why fans can't let it go

    Fans waited nine episodes for Doctor Strange to knock on Westview's door. He almost did.

    What fans think

    Marvel had announced Wanda would appear in Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness, so fans spent WandaVision's run waiting for Strange to arrive, maybe in the finale or a credits scene. He never did. Showrunner Jac Schaeffer later said Strange was planned for the credits tag, trying to teleport Wanda, and an earlier idea had the commercials as his messages. Kevin Feige said it was cut because it would have taken away from Wanda.

    The evidence board

    1. Wanda was announced for the Doctor Strange sequel, so a crossover seemed natural.

      WandaVision, before and during its run

    2. The show was full of 'something is outside the Hex' teases that fans hoped would be Strange.

      WandaVision, episodes 4 to 9

    The case against

    • The finale and its credits scenes show no Strange, and Feige explained why he was cut.

    Debunked

    He does not appear; a planned cameo was cut.
