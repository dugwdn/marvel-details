    Avengers: Doomsday is mostly set on the Fantastic Four's world

    Fans expected the Fantastic Four to come to the Avengers; a newer theory says the Avengers go to Earth-828 instead.

    Why fans can't let it go

    That rocket after the Thunderbolts* credits may not be the Fantastic Four coming to help. It may be a cry for help.

    What fans think

    Thunderbolts* ends with an alert about a retro rocket marked '4' arriving, and many fans took it as the Fantastic Four coming to help against Doom. Then Kevin Feige said Earth-828 would feature heavily in Doomsday, and Inverse argued the rocket is more likely a distress beacon. A Reddit theory in r/MCUTheories (July 2026) says much of the film happens on Earth-828, Doom's home turf, making the Avengers the outsiders in a collapsing world.

    The evidence board

    1. A rocket marked '4' arrives with the Fantastic Four theme playing.

      Thunderbolts*, post-credits scene

    2. Feige said Earth-828 would play a big part in Doomsday.

      Avengers: Doomsday interviews

    3. Sue's narration in the teaser shows the Fantastic Four already knew Doom.

      Avengers: Doomsday teaser (August 2026)

    The case against

    • Avengers Countdown called the Reddit version speculation without a verifiable source.
