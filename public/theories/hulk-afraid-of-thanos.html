    Hulk hid because he's scared of Thanos

    After losing to Thanos, the Hulk refused to come out because he was afraid.

    Why fans can't let it go

    After one beating from Thanos, the Hulk wouldn't come out. Was he really scared?

    What fans think

    Infinity War opens with Thanos beating the Hulk, and for the rest of the film Bruce cannot turn into him. Many viewers decided the Hulk was simply scared. Joe Russo said the real reason is the fight between Bruce and the Hulk for control, which began in Ragnarok. Mark Ruffalo put it this way: 'The only thing Hulk is afraid of is Banner.'

    The evidence board

    1. Thanos beats Hulk, then Bruce cannot transform, even in Wakanda.

      Avengers: Infinity War, opening and Wakanda battle

    2. Unable to become the Hulk, Bruce fights the Wakanda battle inside the Hulkbuster armor.

      Avengers: Infinity War, the battle in Wakanda

    The case against

    • Joe Russo: the two halves are fighting over control, not afraid of Thanos.

    Debunked

    The directors and Mark Ruffalo said Hulk wasn't afraid of Thanos.

    Do you buy it?

    One tap. No account needed.

    Where it started

    We could not find the first post; fans have talked about it since 2018. Common enough that both directors and Ruffalo answered it; covered by SYFY, Den of Geek and Inverse.
