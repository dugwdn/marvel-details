    Still open

    Professor X wiped the world's memory of mutants

    Mutants were always in the main MCU, but Charles Xavier made everyone forget them.

    What fans think

    Fans long asked why no mutants appeared in the MCU's main world. A Reddit theory said Xavier used his telepathy to erase humanity's knowledge of mutants to protect them, and that they would return after the Blip; other versions blame Mr. Sinister or Wanda. Since then the MCU has shown native mutants (Kamala Khan, and Namor, whom actor Tenoch Huerta called a mutant), but no Earth-616 Xavier. Doomsday's X-Men come from another universe.

    The evidence board

    1. Kamala's genes carry a 'mutation'.

      Ms. Marvel episode 6

    2. Tenoch Huerta said his Namor is a mutant.

      Black Panther: Wakanda Forever press

    3. Doomsday's X-Men are from Earth-10005, not the main MCU.

      Avengers: Doomsday premise

    The case against

    • Looper calls hidden mutants unlikely: most mutations are hard to hide and the MCU world is heavily watched.

    Still open

    No main-universe Xavier has appeared, so the idea is neither shown nor ruled out.

    Where it started

    Started on Reddit (u/jadamsmash, per We Got This Covered). Covered by We Got This Covered, WatchMojo's 'craziest theories' list, Looper and Sportskeeda.
