    Debunked

    Maya killed the Kingpin at the end of Hawkeye

    Hawkeye's finale cut away as Maya pointed a gun at Fisk, and a shot rang out off screen.

    Why fans can't let it go

    The camera cut away, a gun went off, and fans thought the Kingpin was gone. His next scene came with an eye patch.

    What fans think

    In Hawkeye's finale, Maya Lopez confronts Wilson Fisk in an alley; the camera cuts away and a gunshot is heard. Many fans decided she killed him, while others noted Fisk had survived worse and recalled a 1998 comic where Maya shoots Kingpin in the face and only blinds him. Echo answered it: Fisk is alive, with an eye patch and damaged sight.

    The evidence board

    1. The gunshot happens off screen, right after Maya raises the gun.

      Hawkeye, episode 6, the alley

    2. Fans were split over whether the off-screen moment was a kill or a fake-out.

      Hawkeye, episode 6

    The case against

    • In the comics Maya's shot only blinded Fisk, and Echo follows that version.

    Debunked

    Echo shows Fisk alive with an injured eye.
