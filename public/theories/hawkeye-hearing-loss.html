    Confirmed

    Hawkeye is losing his hearing

    Like in the comics, Clint Barton's years of explosions have left him hard of hearing.

    Why fans can't let it go

    Years of explosions right next to his ears finally caught up with Clint, just like in the comics.

    What fans think

    In Matt Fraction's comic run, Clint loses most of his hearing, and fans hoped the MCU would follow. Set photos of Jeremy Renner and Hailee Steinfeld near a hearing clinic and trailer shots of Clint wearing a hearing aid fed the theory. The Hawkeye series made it part of the story, and Renner told Jimmy Kimmel he is 'hard of hearing, yeah.'

    The evidence board

    1. Clint wears a hearing aid in the trailer and the series.

      Hawkeye (Disney+, 2021)

    2. Leaked set photos showed the hearing aid.

      Hawkeye set photos

    The case against

    • A side theory that his hearing loss sharpens his aim, like Daredevil's senses, was never shown.

    Confirmed

    The Hawkeye series shows Clint wearing a hearing aid, and Renner confirmed it.

    Do you buy it?

    One tap. No account needed.

    Where it started

    We could not find the first post; fans have talked about it since 2021. Covered by TechRadar, Inverse and MovieWeb.
