    Debunked

    Mysterio really is from another Earth

    Quentin Beck's story about coming from Earth-833 is true and the multiverse has arrived.

    What fans think

    In Far From Home, Mysterio tells Peter and Nick Fury he comes from another Earth destroyed by the Elementals, and fans took it as the MCU's first look at the multiverse. Mid-film, the movie shows Beck is a former Stark employee using drones and illusions. Some fans kept parts of the theory alive, and a few noted the 'Earth-616' name as a clue. Kevin Feige said the reveal just means Beck was lying.

    The evidence board

    1. Beck says he comes from Earth-833 and calls the MCU Earth-616.

      Spider-Man: Far From Home, Beck's story to Peter

    2. Beck calls the MCU's world 'Earth-616', the name later used in Doctor Strange 2; writer Michael Waldron asked 'What did Quentin know?'

      Spider-Man: Far From Home; Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness

    The case against

    • Beck and ex-Stark staff fake the Elemental attacks with drones and illusions.
    • Feige said it just means Beck was lying, not that there is no multiverse.

    Debunked

    The film itself reveals Beck invented his backstory.

    Where it started

    We could not find the first post; fans have talked about it since 2019. Covered by Looper, WhatCulture, We Got This Covered and Inverse.
