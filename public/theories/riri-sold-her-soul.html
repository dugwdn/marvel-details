    Still open

    Riri made a deal with Mephisto too

    Natalie came back to life at the end of Ironheart, and a burn mark on Riri's arm tells fans what it cost.

    Why fans can't let it go

    Riri got her best friend back from the dead. Look at her arm during the hug to see what it cost.

    What fans think

    Ironheart's finale has Mephisto offer Riri a bargain; instead of power, she gets her late best friend Natalie back, in flesh and blood. Mephisto told Parker his price was 'something you won't even miss.' As Riri hugs Natalie, fiery markings like Parker's spread over her forearm. Fans read that as proof Riri now owes Mephisto her soul, and they are waiting to see when he collects.

    The evidence board

    1. Natalie returns as a real person, not the AI.

      Ironheart, finale

    2. Fiery marks like Parker's appear on Riri's arm.

      Ironheart, finale reunion

    The case against

    • Some writers say the show only hints at the deal, and nothing on screen says what Riri gave up.

    Still open

    The show leaves the terms unclear, and no second season has been announced.
