    Confirmed

    Kamala Khan is a mutant

    Ms. Marvel's powers come from her genes, making her one of the MCU's first mutants.

    Why fans can't let it go

    Listen closely in the Ms. Marvel finale: a few notes of an old cartoon theme give away what Kamala is.

    What fans think

    Ms. Marvel changed Kamala's comic origin (Inhuman) to powers unlocked by a family bangle, so fans wondered what was really going on inside her. Many guessed she was a mutant, the MCU's door to the X-Men. In the finale, Bruno tells her there is something different in her genes, 'like a mutation,' while a few notes of the 1990s X-Men cartoon theme play.

    The evidence board

    1. Bruno says her genes have something 'like a mutation' and the X-Men theme plays.

      Ms. Marvel episode 6, 'No Normal', closing minutes

    2. Den of Geek reads the reveal as the first step toward the X-Men entering the main timeline.

      Ms. Marvel episode 6

    The case against

    • Some outlets say the line only hints; Marvel has not built a full mutant storyline around her yet.

    Confirmed

    The Ms. Marvel finale says her genes carry a mutation and plays the X-Men theme.

    Where it started

    We could not find the first post; fans have talked about it since 2022. Gizmodo called her 'the first official mutant of the MCU'; covered by SYFY, Den of Geek, Inverse.
