    Confirmed

    The asterisk in Thunderbolts* means 'New Avengers'

    Marvel would not explain the asterisk; fans guessed the team would get a new name by the end.

    Why fans can't let it go

    Marvel stuck an asterisk on a movie title and refused to explain it. Fans cracked it before opening night.

    What fans think

    When Marvel retitled the film Thunderbolts*, it refused to say what the asterisk meant. Fans pointed to the line 'The Avengers are not coming' and guessed the team would be rebranded. A Marvel Japan poster added fine print reading 'The Avengers are not available.' In the film's ending, Valentina introduces the team to the press as the New Avengers. Director Jake Schreier said that ending was the plan from his first pitch.

    The evidence board

    1. Trailers stress that the Avengers are not coming.

      Thunderbolts* trailers

    2. A Japanese poster footnote said the Avengers are not available.

      Thunderbolts* Marvel Japan poster

    The case against

    • Much of the answer came out through marketing and early screening reports before the film, and those reports disagreed on details such as which poster carried the footnote.

    Confirmed

    The ending names them the New Avengers, and Schreier said it was always the plan.

    Where it started

    Started on X in 2024-2025. Covered by Inverse, IMDb News, WDW News Today and Soap Central.
