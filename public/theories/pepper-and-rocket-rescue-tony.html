    Debunked

    Pepper and Rocket will rescue Tony from space

    Tony was stranded in space with Nebula, and fans picked Pepper, in her Rescue armor, to bring him home.

    Why fans can't let it go

    Tony was dying in space and fans knew who'd come: Pepper, in her own armor. They were half right.

    What fans think

    Endgame's first trailer showed Tony adrift in the Benatar, recording a goodbye for Pepper. A Reddit post by Dresudi, covered by We Got This Covered, said Pepper would suit up and fly out with Rocket to save him, a fitting job for the comics' Rescue. A leaked photo of Gwyneth Paltrow in armor added fuel. Others bet on Captain Marvel. In the film, Carol Danvers carries the ship home; Pepper does wear her armor, but in the final battle.

    The evidence board

    1. Tony records a farewell message as the ship runs out of air.

      Avengers: Endgame trailer

    2. A leaked photo seemed to show Paltrow in the Rescue armor.

      Avengers: Endgame set photo

    The case against

    • Looper argued Stark Industries had never shown any interest in space travel, so Pepper had no way to reach him.

    Debunked

    Captain Marvel rescues Tony and Nebula; Pepper's armor appears later.

    One tap. No account needed.

    Where it started

    Started on Reddit in 2018-12 (u/Dresudi). Covered by We Got This Covered, Reactor, Nerdist, Inverse and El Espectador.
