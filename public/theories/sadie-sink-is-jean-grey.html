    Confirmed

    Sadie Sink's secret role is Jean Grey

    Sadie Sink's mystery character in Spider-Man: Brand New Day is the X-Men's Jean Grey.

    What fans think

    Marvel and Sony kept Sadie Sink's role secret for about a year, and fans settled on Jean Grey: red hair, a telepath rumor and the X-Men's expected arrival. Tom Holland and Sink dodged the question, which reminded fans of Andrew Garfield's denials before No Way Home. The film reveals she is Jean Grey, a telepath body-hopping through New York while searching for her kidnapped sister Sara.

    The evidence board

    1. Jean Grey is the telepath possessing people around Peter, hunting for her sister.

      Spider-Man: Brand New Day, revealed over an hour in

    2. Her role stayed secret before release, like the extra Spider-Men in No Way Home.

      Pre-release marketing

    The case against

    • Before release Tom Holland told a fan Jean Grey was not in the film; director Destin Daniel Cretton also denied she was there to set up an X-Men film.

    Confirmed

    The released film and its cast list name Sink's character as Jean Grey.

    Where it started

    We could not find the first post; fans have talked about it since 2025. Year-long guessing game covered by tbreak, PinkNews, Kinocheck and others; called 'the leading fan theory' before release.
