    The rat that saved the universe met Ant-Man years before

    A Reddit theory says the Endgame rat that freed Scott is the same rat he ran into in Ant-Man.

    Why fans can't let it go

    Endgame's real hero might be a rat, and one fan thinks it met Scott Lang years earlier.

    What fans think

    Endgame's turning point is a rat stepping on the controls of Luis's van and freeing Scott from the Quantum Realm. On the commentary, the filmmakers joked it was 'the hero of the Infinity Saga.' Reddit user u/iiMCUii argued it was the same rat Scott bumps into the first time he shrinks in Ant-Man, watching over him for years. A commenter answered that the longest-living rat on record lasted 7 years and 4 months.

    The evidence board

    1. Scott runs into a rat right after first using his suit.

      Ant-Man, Scott's first shrink

    2. A rat walks over the van's controls and brings Scott back.

      Avengers: Endgame, storage unit

    The case against

    • The two films are about eight years apart, longer than any recorded rat has lived.

    Still open

    No one at Marvel has said whether it is the same rat; the directors only joked that the rat saved the universe.

    Do you buy it?

    One tap. No account needed.

    Where it started

    Started on Reddit in 2019-10 (u/iiMCUii, r/MarvelStudios). Covered by ComicBook.com, WhatCulture, We Got This Covered and Kino.de.
