    Confirmed

    There is a third man inside Marc Spector

    Steven and Marc both black out, yet someone keeps fighting; fans said it was the comics' Jake Lockley.

    Why fans can't let it go

    Steven and Marc both black out in fights, yet someone keeps winning them. Who is driving?

    What fans think

    In Moon Knight, Steven Grant and Marc Spector share one body, but some fights end with both of them blacked out and the enemies beaten. Fans pointed to mirror shots, credit images with three faces, and a third sarcophagus in the episode 4 asylum, and named Jake Lockley, the third personality from the comics. The post-credits scene of episode 6 confirms him: Jake takes over, speaks Spanish and works for Khonshu.

    The evidence board

    1. Both Marc and Steven lose time during a fight, and someone else wins it.

      Moon Knight, episode 3 in Cairo

    2. A closed sarcophagus sits beside the ones holding Marc and Steven.

      Moon Knight, episode 4 asylum hallway

    3. Mirror reflections seem to hold one figure too many.

      Moon Knight, episodes 1 and 2

    The case against

    • Not every clue was planned: some 'extra reflection' shots were just the show's mirror style, and Jake's name is only spoken in the final scene.

    Confirmed

    The finale's post-credits scene introduces Jake Lockley.
