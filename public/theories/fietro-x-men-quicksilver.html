    WandaVision's Pietro is the X-Men's Quicksilver

    Evan Peters' Pietro is the Fox X-Men Quicksilver, opening the door to the mutants.

    Why fans can't let it go

    Wanda's brother came back with an X-Men actor's face, and fans thought the multiverse had opened. It was a joke.

    What fans think

    When Wanda's dead brother arrived played by Evan Peters, the Quicksilver of Fox's X-Men films, fans thought the multiverse had brought the X-Men into the MCU. The finale revealed he was Ralph Bohner, Agnes's never-seen husband, under Agatha's spell. Jac Schaeffer said the casting was there to set up a joke about the surname, not the multiverse.

    The evidence board

    1. Wanda's brother turns up played by the X-Men films' Quicksilver actor.

      WandaVision episode 5, end

    2. Wanda looks truly shocked when he arrives, and he has little in common with the Age of Ultron Pietro apart from his speed.

      WandaVision episode 5, the doorstep scene

    The case against

    • He was Ralph, hypnotized into thinking he was Pietro.

    Debunked

    Episode 9 reveals 'Pietro' is Ralph Bohner under a spell.

    Do you buy it?

    One tap. No account needed.

    Where it started

    We could not find the first post; fans have talked about it since 2021. Huge online reaction to the casting; covered by ScreenCrush, Inverse and Looper.
