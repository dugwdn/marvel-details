    Confirmed

    Groot's last words meant 'Dad'

    When Groot turns to dust in Infinity War, his final 'I am Groot' to Rocket had a meaning, and James Gunn shared it.

    Why fans can't let it go

    Groot's last 'I am Groot' in Infinity War meant one word. The director told a fan what it was.

    What fans think

    In Infinity War's snap, teenage Groot fades away saying one last 'I am Groot' while looking at Rocket. Rocket raised Groot after the first film, so fans guessed the line was something like 'Dad'. In May 2018 a fan asked James Gunn on Twitter, and Gunn answered with one word: 'Dad.' He apologized for the heartbreak and said the line was in the script. Reports also say Vin Diesel is given Groot's lines written out in English.

    The evidence board

    1. Groot looks at Rocket, says 'I am Groot', and turns to dust.

      Avengers: Infinity War, the snap in Wakanda

    2. Rocket looks after baby and teen Groot through Guardians Vol. 2, so a father-son bond was already on screen.

      Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2 and its credits scenes

    3. Vin Diesel reportedly gets Groot's lines written in English, so every 'I am Groot' has a meaning.

      Behind the scenes of the Guardians films

    The case against

    • The meaning comes from a tweet, not the film; the movie never translates the line, so some say it should stay open to each viewer.

    Confirmed

    James Gunn answered a fan on Twitter in May 2018: the line was 'Dad'.

    Do you buy it?

    One tap. No account needed.

    Where it started

    Started on Twitter in 2018-05 (a fan, Joe Green, asked Gunn on May 6, 2018). Covered by TheWrap, ScreenCrush, Inverse, Looper and Fox News.
