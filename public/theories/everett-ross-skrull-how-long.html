    Everett Ross was a Skrull long before Secret Invasion

    Secret Invasion revealed Ross as a Skrull; fans dug back through Black Panther looking for when he was swapped.

    Why fans can't let it go

    Secret Invasion revealed Agent Ross as a Skrull in its first episode. Fans are still rewatching Black Panther for the swap.

    What fans think

    Secret Invasion's first episode shows Everett K. Ross, the CIA agent from Civil War and the Black Panther films, as a Skrull who dies during an escape. The finale frees the real Ross from a Skrull prison. Fans now argue when the swap happened: some say after Wakanda Forever, others say he was a Skrull all along. Martin Freeman said he did not play Ross as a Skrull in Wakanda Forever.

    The evidence board

    1. Ross is shown as a Skrull and dies fleeing Talos.

      Secret Invasion, episode 1

    2. /Film went through Ross's appearances to work out when the switch might have happened.

      Civil War, Black Panther and Wakanda Forever

    The case against

    • Martin Freeman said he did not play Ross as a Skrull in Wakanda Forever.

    Still open
