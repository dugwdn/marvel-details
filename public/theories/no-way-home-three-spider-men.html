    Confirmed

    Tobey and Andrew are in No Way Home

    Spider-Man: No Way Home brings back Tobey Maguire's and Andrew Garfield's Spider-Men.

    What fans think

    Once No Way Home confirmed villains from the older films (Doctor Octopus, Electro), fans concluded the earlier Spider-Men had to come too. They studied trailers for edited-out figures and watched every denial. Andrew Garfield said repeatedly he wasn't involved. The film brought both back, side by side with Tom Holland.

    The evidence board

    1. Alfred Molina's Doctor Octopus and Jamie Foxx's Electro were confirmed, pointing at their universes.

      No Way Home trailers

    2. Leaked set photos and videos plus oddly edited trailer shots made fans think the older Spider-Men were cut out of the footage.

      No Way Home trailers and set leaks

    3. A trailer shot of MJ falling from scaffolding echoed Gwen Stacy's death in The Amazing Spider-Man 2; in the film, Garfield's Peter catches her.

      Spider-Man: No Way Home trailer, then the final battle

    The case against

    • Garfield denied it on the record several times before release.

    Confirmed

    Both actors appear in the finished film.
