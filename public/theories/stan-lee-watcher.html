    Partly true

    Stan Lee was one character all along

    All of Stan Lee's cameos are the same being, someone who works for the Watchers.

    What fans think

    Stan Lee turned up in nearly every MCU film as a different person: a mailman, a bus passenger, a FedEx driver. Fans guessed that this was one character watching Earth, most often Uatu the Watcher from the comics. Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2 answered with a scene of Lee in a spacesuit chatting with three Watchers about 'that time I was a FedEx deliveryman.' James Gunn said fan theories inspired the scene, and Lee is credited as a Watcher informant rather than a Watcher.

    The evidence board

    1. Lee sits with Watchers and mentions his FedEx job.

      Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2, a mid-credits scene

    2. The FedEx job is his Civil War cameo, delivering Steve's package to Tony.

      Captain America: Civil War, near the end

    The case against

    • His line about FedEx refers to Civil War, which had not happened yet in Vol. 2's timeline; Gunn admitted the slip.

    Partly true

    Vol. 2 shows Lee reporting to the Watchers, but he is an informant, not Uatu, and the scene leaves the details loose.

    Do you buy it?

    One tap. No account needed.

    Where it started

    Started on Twitter and fan forums (Gunn said he saw it in fan theories). Long-running theory that both James Gunn and Kevin Feige addressed in public; covered by Inverse, Looper and ComicBookMovie.
