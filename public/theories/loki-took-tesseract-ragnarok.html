    Confirmed

    Loki stole the Tesseract while Asgard burned

    In Ragnarok, Loki stops in Odin's vault and eyes the Tesseract; fans said he took it, and Infinity War shows it on the refugee ship.

    Why fans can't let it go

    In Ragnarok, Loki pauses in Odin's vault and stares at a glowing cube. Infinity War's first scene shows what he did next.

    What fans think

    While the heroes flee Asgard in Thor: Ragnarok, Loki goes down to Odin's treasure room to wake Surtur and pauses in front of the glowing Tesseract. The film never shows him pick it up, so fans argued he pocketed it. The Infinity War trailers showed Loki holding out the Tesseract, which kept the theory going, though some writers said the trailer did not prove a betrayal. Infinity War opens on the Asgardian refugee ship with the Tesseract aboard: Loki brings it out to save Thor, and Thanos takes the Space Stone from it.

    The evidence board

    1. Loki stops and looks at the Tesseract in Odin's vault before Surtur rises.

      Thor: Ragnarok, Odin's treasure room during the escape

    2. The Infinity War teaser shows Loki offering the Tesseract to someone off screen.

      Avengers: Infinity War teaser trailer

    3. SYFY's weekly theory roundup covered fans tracing Loki's 'Infinity Stone quest' from Ragnarok forward.

      Thor: Ragnarok ending and Infinity War trailers

    The case against

    • Some writers argued the trailer did not show Loki betraying Thor, only holding the cube, so the 'he stole it to sell out Thor' version went too far.
