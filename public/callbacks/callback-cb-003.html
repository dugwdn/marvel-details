    Obadiah Stane's Betrayal

    plot-twist

    ⚡ Foreshadowed In

    Iron Man (2008)
    Scene: Tony's return from Afghanistan

    Obadiah Stane, Howard Stark's old partner who runs the company, warns Tony that quitting weapons will ruin Stark Industries, and keeps asking about Tony's new work.

    ✓ Fulfilled In

    Iron Man (2008)
    Scene: Stane's attack at Tony's home

    Stane is revealed to have been selling weapons to the Ten Rings and hiring them to kill Tony. He steals the reactor from Tony's chest to power his own armor.

    💡 Analysis

    The father figure who seemed to be protecting the company was the one who arranged Tony's capture. Both halves happen inside the first film.

    Source: Wikipedia: Iron Man (2008 film), Plot
