      Questions about the quiz

      How hard is the Marvel quiz?

      There are four levels: Sidekick (easy), Hero (medium), Avenger (hard) and Inevitable (insanely hard, with a 15-second timer). Each round is 10 questions picked at random from 82 total.

      How do quiz points work?

      Each right answer earns the level's points (10, 20, 40 or 75). Three or more right in a row adds a 50% streak bonus, and a perfect round adds five answers' worth of bonus points. Points add up to your quiz rank, from Recruit to Inevitable.

      Do I need an account to play?

      No. Anyone can play and your points are kept on this device. Sign in with Google if you want your points and rank on every device.

      Where do the answers come from?

      Every question names the film where you can check it and the scene it comes from. Questions come from 21 films: Iron Man (2008), Iron Man 2 (2010), Captain America: The First Avenger (2011), Thor (2011), The Avengers (2012), Iron Man 3 (2013), Captain America: The Winter Soldier (2014), Guardians of the Galaxy (2014), Ant-Man (2015), Avengers: Age of Ultron (2015), Captain America: Civil War (2016), Doctor Strange (2016), Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2 (2017), Spider-Man: Homecoming (2017), Thor: Ragnarok (2017), Avengers: Infinity War (2018), Black Panther (2018), Avengers: Endgame (2019), Captain Marvel (2019), Spider-Man: Far From Home (2019), Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings (2021).
