    • Iron Man (Anthony Edward Stark), played by Robert Downey Jr.: From self-centered industrialist to selfless savior willing to sacrifice everything for others.
    • Captain America (Steven Grant Rogers), played by Chris Evans: From frozen relic to modern leader who must balance old ideals with new realities.
    • Thor (Thor Odinson), played by Chris Hemsworth: From arrogant warrior prince to humble defender willing to sacrifice his throne for love and duty.
    • Loki (Loki Laufeyson), played by Tom Hiddleston: From resentful outcast seeking approval to ambitious villain tasting power and responsibility.
    • Black Widow (Natasha Alianovna Romanoff), played by Scarlett Johansson: From lethal assassin seeking redemption to trusted hero proving her worth beyond her training.
    • Hawkeye (Clinton Barton), played by Jeremy Renner: From loyal soldier following orders to independent hero making his own moral choices.
    • Hulk (Robert Bruce Banner), played by Mark Ruffalo: From scientist afraid of his own power to hero learning to work with it toward noble ends.
    • Nick Fury (Nicholas Joseph Fury), played by Samuel L. Jackson: From shadowy spymaster to visionary leader building Earth's mightiest defense.
    • Pepper Potts (Virginia Potts), played by Gwyneth Paltrow: From assistant to CEO to the human heart that keeps Iron Man grounded.
    • Happy Hogan (Harold Joseph Hogan), played by Jon Favreau: From boxer-turned-bodyguard to loyal friend and unexpected hero.
    • Thanos, played by Josh Brolin: A warlord convinced that wiping out half of all life is mercy, who gets exactly what he wanted and then loses it.
    • Spider-Man (Peter Parker), played by Tom Holland: A kid from Queens who wants to be an Avenger, then learns what it costs.
    • War Machine (James Rupert Rhodes), played by Don Cheadle: Tony's best friend and the military's man in the suit, always a step from the spotlight.
    • Captain Marvel (Carol Danvers), played by Brie Larson: A pilot who learns her power was always her own, then carries it into the fight for half the universe.
    • Doctor Strange (Stephen Strange), played by Benedict Cumberbatch: An arrogant surgeon who learns to bet everything on the one future that works.
    • Black Panther (T'Challa), played by Chadwick Boseman: A king who turns from revenge and opens a hidden nation to the world.
    • Scarlet Witch (Wanda Maximoff), played by Elizabeth Olsen: A girl made into a weapon whose power grows with every loss.
    • Ant-Man (Scott Lang), played by Paul Rudd: An ex-con who wants to be a good dad and ends up holding the key to undoing the snap.
    • Nebula, played by Karen Gillan: Thanos's abused daughter, rebuilt piece by piece, who helps beat him.
    • Obadiah Stane, played by Jeff Bridges: Tony's mentor and business partner who wanted the company, and the armor, for himself.
