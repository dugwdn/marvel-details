    How do I rank up?

    Every deleted scene, callback and rabbit hole is a hidden detail. A deleted scene counts once its card has been on your screen for a moment; a callback or rabbit hole counts when you open it. The more you find, the higher your rank, from Civilian all the way to The Watcher, who has found every one.

      Rank names are characters from the films, used as fan titles. This site is not affiliated with Marvel or Disney.

      What does "Hide spoilers" do?

      Mark the movies you've seen. With the switch on, details from a movie you haven't marked are blurred on movie pages, deleted scenes and callbacks. Tap a blurred detail to read it anyway. Rabbit holes aren't blurred because each one covers many films, some outside our five.

      What do you keep about me?

      Without an account, everything stays in this browser on this device; nothing is sent to us. If you sign in (Google, Facebook or X, on this page or the sign-in page), we keep the id and first name that account gives us, a sign-in cookie, and this list so it can follow you to other devices. Never your email or password. Deleting your account also deletes the list we keep. More on the privacy page.

      This empties your rank, seen movies, saved list and favorites in this browser. If you're signed in, sign out first or they'll come back from your account.
