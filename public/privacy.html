Privacy

Updated October 6, 2026.

Reading the site

You can read everything here without an account. Like most sites, we count visits with Google Analytics, and Google AdSense shows ads that may use cookies to pick what you see. You can turn off personalized ads at Google's ad settings.

If you make an account

You can sign in with Google, Facebook or X. We keep only the id and first name that account gives us, plus a sign-in cookie on your device. We never get your password, we don't keep your email, and we never post anything for you. Accounts are for people 13 or older.

My Marvel: your lists

The movies you mark as seen, your saved list, your favorite characters, the hidden details you've found, your rank and your spoiler setting are kept in your own browser on this device and are not sent to us. If you're signed in, we also keep a copy with your account (up to 16 KB) so they follow you to your other devices. "Clear my list on this device" on My Marvel empties the browser copy.

Deleting your data

Go to Sign in, open "Delete my account" and tap the button. Your account, its sign-ins and the copy of your lists are deleted right away. Signing out only ends the sign-in on that device.
